## In three lines

- MHLW proposed FY2027 changes for home-visit care and home nursing, including travel-cost fee categories in place of reductions and online check-ins by service coordinators. Proposals, not decisions.[^1][^2]
- For care management it proposed abolishing add-on (A) and the 40% heavy-care rule in add-on (I), and a reduction for offices staffing care managers who skip the new mandatory training.[^3]
- Thailand's DHSS kept the FY2027 stipend quota for village health volunteers at 1,075,163.[^5]

## Home-visit services (271st meeting, 19 Oct 2026)

On 19 October 2026 the 271st meeting of the {{< ja "社会保障審議会介護給付費分科会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo kyūfuhi bunkakai" "Social Security Council, Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee" >}} tabled proposals for home-visit services and care management.[^1] For {{< ja "訪問介護" "hōmon kaigo" "home-visit care" >}}: the {{< ja "初回加算" "shokai kasan" "first-visit add-on" >}} would also cover a {{< ja "サービス提供責任者" "sābisu teikyō sekininsha" "service coordinator" >}} visiting to assess when writing a new plan; online check-ins by the coordinator during a helper's visit could be counted; and travel-cost fee categories would replace the current {{< ja "減算" "gensan" "reduction" >}} approach.[^1] For {{< ja "訪問看護" "hōmon kango" "home-visit nursing" >}}: night add-ons on the month's first night visit even with the {{< ja "緊急時訪問看護加算" "kinkyūji hōmon kango kasan" "emergency home-nursing add-on" >}}, and new fees for assisting online consultations and for ICT data-sharing with medical institutions.[^2] See [the money, the workforce, and what 2027 and 2040 look like]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}) and [the monthly cycle]({{< relref "guides/monthly-cycle-plan-to-payment" >}}).

## Care management

For {{< ja "居宅介護支援" "kyotaku kaigo shien" "care management (care-plan services)" >}}: abolish {{< ja "特定事業所加算" "tokutei jigyōsho kasan" "specific-office add-on" >}} (A) with a one-year transition, and drop the 40% heavy-care requirement from (I); office managers would no longer need to be a {{< ja "主任介護支援専門員" "shunin kaigo shien senmon'in" "chief care manager" >}}, only a care manager with five years' experience; consent for online monitoring could be handled more flexibly. Following the June 2026 law that abolished licence renewal, offices staffing care managers who skip periodic training without good reason would face a reduction.[^3] See [Who does what]({{< relref "guides/who-does-what" >}}).

Next: the {{< ja "社会保障審議会介護保険部会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo hoken bukai" "Social Security Council, LTC Insurance Committee" >}} meets on 26 October 2026 on benefits and burden and on {{< ja "要介護認定" "yō-kaigo nintei" "care-need certification" >}}.[^4]

## Thailand

On 19 October 2026 DHSS said its allocation committee kept the FY2027 (B.E. 2570) quota of village health volunteers (อสม.) entitled to the stipend at 1,075,163, unchanged, with more monitoring of their work.[^5] See [Who does what in Thai community care]({{< relref "/thailand/who-does-what-community-care" >}}).

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: these proposals reward software that records how work was done: online check-ins, ICT sharing with clinics, and care managers' training status. Abolishing add-on (A) and the 40% rule changes add-on logic that care-management software calculates today. Thailand's flat quota of about 1.08 million stipend volunteers is a stable user base for any Smart อสม.-style tool.
