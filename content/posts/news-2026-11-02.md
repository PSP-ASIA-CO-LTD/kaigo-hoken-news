## Highlights

- **Registered-facility care support and five-band regional grades (272nd, 28 Oct).** MHLW drafted a care manager plus a {{< ja "地域生活相談員" "chiiki seikatsu sōdan'in" "community life counsellor" >}} (dual-hat allowed) and a fee like home-care support; {{< ja "地域区分" "chiiki kubun" "regional grade (fee-area class)" >}} would follow the five-band civil-service allowance, with a transition to the end of fiscal 2029. Proposals, not decisions.[^1][^2]
- **Facility add-ons and dialysis (273rd, 2 Nov).** For special nursing homes: rewrite the dialysis-transport add-on as a support package, merge five low-uptake discharge add-ons, abolish two more. Parallel dialysis and tidy-up drafts for geriatric health facilities and integrated care hospitals.[^3][^4][^5]
- **Thailand: Smart อสม. Plus is live.** DHSS said the new volunteer app and Work Report are in use from October 2026; the old Smart อสม. app closes on 30 November 2026.[^6]

## 1. Housing services and regional grades (272nd, 28 Oct 2026)

The 272nd meeting of the {{< ja "社会保障審議会介護給付費分科会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo kyūfuhi bunkakai" "Social Security Council, Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee" >}} drafted FY2027 rules for {{< ja "登録施設介護支援" "tōroku shisetsu kaigo shien" "registered-facility care support" >}}: staff a care manager and a community life counsellor, write the plan using information from the home, and set the basic fee like home-care support, adjusted for community-life consultation and congregate housing. Start is assumed after the paid-home registration transition ends.[^1] For {{< ja "特定施設入居者生活介護" "tokutei shisetsu nyūkyosha seikatsu kaigo" "specified-facility daily-life care" >}}, MHLW would survey residential-home conversions and study the external-service type's missing add-ons.[^7] Regional grades would follow the five-band civil-service allowance, let municipalities keep the old top-up through fiscal 2029, and replace the 4- and 5-grade-gap rules with a 3-grade-gap rule.[^2] See [private elderly housing]({{< relref "guides/private-pay-elderly-housing" >}}) and [the money, the workforce, and what 2027 and 2040 look like]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}).

## 2. Facilities (273rd, 2 Nov 2026)

The 273rd meeting, on 2 November, drafted FY2027 changes for the three insurance facilities. For {{< ja "介護老人福祉施設" "kaigo rōjin fukushi shisetsu" "special nursing home (tokuyō)" >}}: rewrite the dialysis-transport add-on as a support package; merge five discharge add-ons; abolish two more (one-year transition); and, outside islands and depopulated areas, revise the transitional small-home basic fee.[^3] For {{< ja "介護老人保健施設" "kaigo rōjin hoken shisetsu" "geriatric health services facility (rōken)" >}}: extend initial add-on (I) beyond acute-care general wards; rewrite the family-doctor medication add-on (LIFE's drug-info score is deferred).[^4] For {{< ja "介護医療院" "kaigo iryō-in" "integrated medical and long-term care facility" >}}: a dialysis-acceptance evaluation, and a merge of three low-uptake discharge add-ons.[^5] See [the money, the workforce, and what 2027 and 2040 look like]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}).

## 3. Thailand: Smart อสม. Plus

On 27 October DHSS said {{< th "สมาร์ท อสม. Plus" "Smart OSM Plus" "Smart village-health-volunteer Plus app" >}} and the Work Report information system are available from October 2026 on the App Store and Google Play. Province and district staff must confirm the data before it is used for indicator reports and the volunteer stipend. The old Smart อสม. app closes on 30 November 2026.[^6] See [Who does what in Thai community care]({{< relref "/thailand/who-does-what-community-care" >}}) and [Thai government care systems]({{< relref "/thailand/competitors/government-systems" >}}).

## Also this week

SMS's 29 October H1 results put Kaipoke and Kabenashi Cloud at 63,900 offices and 28,300 corporations at end-September (ARPA ¥123,000, ARR ¥14.0 billion).[^8] Wiseman restarted the MeLL+ apps between 27 and 31 October; voice-record AI and Care-Repo were still down.[^9] ND Software finished its PHP (php2shell) maintenance at 10:10 JST on 27 October, no damage confirmed.[^10] From 29 October Kyōtango City has some care-information-platform functions usable.[^11] See [Kaipoke]({{< relref "competitors/kaipoke" >}}), [Wiseman]({{< relref "competitors/wiseman" >}}), [Honobono NEXT]({{< relref "competitors/honobono-next" >}}) and [the Kaigo WEB Service API guide]({{< relref "guides/kaigo-web-service-api" >}}).
