## Highlights

- **Platform start dates slip.** The Kaigo Kiban Portal update of 10 November (status as of 5 November) still lists 4 cities live on the {{< ja "介護情報基盤" "kaigo jōhō kiban" "care information platform" >}}. Municipalities with a published start date fell to 1,523, from 1,542 on 4 November. Eleven Hokkaido municipalities that were due to start on 13 November moved back to "scheduling".[^1][^2] See [Kaigo WEB Service and its API]({{< relref "guides/kaigo-web-service-api" >}}).
- **Kanamic's full year.** For the year to September 2026, revenue was ¥6,164 million (+12.1%) and operating profit ¥2,057 million (+28.1%).[^3] It reports 62,684 offices and 406,879 user IDs.[^4] It raised the year-end dividend to ¥9.50.[^5] See [Kanamic]({{< relref "competitors/kanamic" >}}).
- **SMS recruits abroad.** On 13 November SMS (Kaipoke) launched "Kaigo Job Global", a referral service for {{< ja "特定技能" "tokutei ginō" "Specified Skilled Worker" >}} care staff. It starts in Tokyo and three nearby prefectures, with an Indonesian partner school.[^6] See [foreign care workers]({{< relref "guides/foreign-care-workers-and-multilingual-software" >}}).

## Also this week

- **SmaCare Lite** passed the Care Plan Data Exchange vendor test (notice of 13 November).[^7] See [SmaCare]({{< relref "competitors/smacare" >}}).
- **Training schools.** On 9 November MHLW named NTT Data Institute of Management Consulting to run its model project for ICT and Japanese teaching at {{< ja "介護福祉士養成施設" "kaigo fukushishi yōsei shisetsu" "certified care worker training schools" >}}.[^8] Support teams help MHLW-chosen model schools pick ICT equipment and plan lessons with it.[^9] See [care worker training schools]({{< relref "guides/care-worker-training-schools" >}}).
- **Coming up, 16 November.** The 275th {{< ja "介護給付費分科会" "kaigo kyūfuhi bunkakai" "Subcommittee on Long-Term Care Benefit Costs" >}} takes up cross-cutting FY2027 issues: {{< ja "処遇改善" "shogū kaizen" "pay improvement" >}} for care staff and productivity.[^10] Its survey committee will see the FY2026 {{< ja "介護事業経営実態調査" "kaigo jigyō keiei jittai chōsa" "survey on the economic conditions of care businesses" >}} results.[^11]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: the Hokkaido slips are a reminder that platform go-live is municipality by municipality, so software must hold a per-municipality start date and keep existing routes running until then. Kanamic's growth in offices and IDs shows the value of a network sold to whole districts. SMS and Wiseman now both pair software with foreign-worker recruitment. For PSP Asia, the MHLW model project is worth watching: its casebook may show what ICT training schools buy.
