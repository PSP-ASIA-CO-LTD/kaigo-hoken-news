## Highlights

- **Same-building fees.** On 20 November the {{< ja "財政制度等審議会" "zaisei seido-tō shingikai" "Fiscal System Council" >}} recommended that the FY2027 fee revision pay less for {{< ja "集中提供型" "shūchū teikyō-gata" "concentrated provision" >}}, care from an office inside or next to a housing block.[^1] For care management it named the {{< ja "同一建物減算" "dōitsu tatemono gensan" "same-building reduction" >}}, the {{< ja "特定事業所集中減算" "tokutei jigyōsho shūchū gensan" "specified-office concentration reduction" >}} and the {{< ja "特定事業所加算" "tokutei jigyōsho kasan" "specified-office add-on" >}}, or one lower fee table that already includes them.[^1] The same cut covers home help, day care, home rehabilitation, home medical-management guidance and home-visit nursing.[^1]
- **Show that the exchange was used.** Comparing the FY2023 and FY2025 care-business surveys, the council found no statistically significant link between adopting care technology and either productivity (users per care worker) or wages.[^2] It said the revision should look again at making actual use of the {{< ja "ケアプランデータ連携システム" "kea puran dēta renkei shisutemu" "Care Plan Data Exchange" >}} a condition of the add-on.[^2] See [Care Plan Data Exchange]({{< relref "guides/care-plan-data-exchange" >}}).
- **A wider 20% charge.** The 20% user-charge band has stood since 2018. The council said widen it, and reach a conclusion soon under the 2026 Basic Policy.[^3] It cited pension income of 2.3–2.6 million yen, a band already put forward at the end of last year.[^3] It also said care management should carry a user charge. A charge for {{< ja "登録施設介護支援" "tōroku shisetsu kaigo shien" "registered-facility care support" >}} is already planned within FY2028.[^4] This is a recommendation to the finance minister. The health ministry has not adopted it. See [the money and 2027]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}).

## Also in the recommendation

Home-visit nursing offices where therapists are more than half the staff still showed a balance rate of 14.1%.[^5] The council said staffing add-ons should be smaller and easier to meet, so that keeping headcount does not cancel a gain from technology, and that fees should move toward outcomes such as a lower care level and a return home.[^6] It also said home help and day care for care levels 1 and 2 should move into {{< ja "地域支援事業" "chiiki shien jigyō" "community support projects" >}}, as support-level services already have.[^7]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: a claim engine that prices a visit the same inside a housing block and out on the road is what this recommendation is aimed at. If the health ministry follows it, same-building reductions get deeper, and an office would have to show it used the Care Plan Data Exchange. A wider 20% charge is an income test at the municipality. A user charge on care management would be a new claim line.
