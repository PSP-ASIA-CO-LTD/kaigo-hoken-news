## Highlights

- **Welfare-equipment comms from 1 February 2027.** Vol.1549 keeps maintenance, use and location alerts payable on listed devices; a health-status alert makes the whole product non-payable; comms fees stay private.[^1]
- **FY2027 housing and unit-price drafts.** The 272nd meeting (28 Oct) proposed excretion-plan evaluation in specified facilities, registered-facility care support at 44 users per care manager (49 with the exchange), and a regional-classification transition through FY2029. Proposals, not decisions.[^2][^3][^4]
- **FY2027 facility drafts; fourth live city.** The 273rd meeting (2 Nov) proposed a full dialysis-support evaluation in special nursing homes and counting tokuyō discharges in the rōken home-return rate.[^5][^6] Kyōtango City went live on 29 October.[^7]

## 1. Welfare-equipment comms (Vol.1549, 26 Oct)

{{< ja "介護保険最新情報" "kaigo hoken saishin jōhō" "LTC insurance 'latest information' notice" >}} Vol.1549 (老高発1026第1号) amends the 2000 notice on what {{< ja "福祉用具" "fukushi yōgu" "welfare equipment" >}} insurance will pay, from **1 February 2027**. Where comms cannot be split off, alerts that help maintain, repair or track listed power-assist wheelchairs, ordinary powered wheelchairs, electric-assist attachments, special beds and wander-alert devices stay payable. A product that notifies health-status changes is non-payable as a whole. Comms fees stay outside the benefit.[^1] See [Who does what]({{< relref "guides/who-does-what" >}}).

## 2. FY2027 drafts (272nd and 273rd)

The 272nd {{< ja "社会保障審議会介護給付費分科会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo kyūfuhi bunkakai" "Social Security Council, Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee" >}} proposed evaluating per-resident excretion-support plans in {{< ja "特定施設入居者生活介護" "tokutei shisetsu nyūkyosha seikatsu kaigo" "specified-facility daily-life care" >}}.[^2] For {{< ja "登録施設介護支援" "tōroku shisetsu kaigo shien" "registered-facility care support" >}}: one full-time care manager per 44 users (49 with the Care Plan Data Exchange), plus a community-life counsellor who may be the same person; start after the paid-home registration transition ends.[^3] {{< ja "地域区分" "chiiki kubun" "regional classification (unit-price area)" >}} would still follow the civil-servant regional allowance, with a municipal-choice transition through the end of fiscal 2029.[^4] The 273rd meeting proposed rewriting the {{< ja "特別通院送迎加算" "tokubetsu tsūin sōgei kasan" "special outpatient-transport add-on" >}} as a full dialysis-support package, and counting discharges to special nursing homes in the geriatric-health-facility home-return rate.[^5][^6] See [the money and 2027]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}) and [private elderly housing]({{< relref "guides/private-pay-elderly-housing" >}}).

## Also this week

Kyōtango City became the fourth municipality on the {{< ja "介護情報基盤" "kaigo jōhō kiban" "care information platform" >}} on 29 October. The 4 November history update lists 4 cities live and 1,542 with dates.[^7] See [Kaigo WEB Service]({{< relref "guides/kaigo-web-service-api" >}}). The Care Plan Data Exchange help desk is moving to the Kaigo Kiban Portal; from January 2027 questions go to 0120-697-312.[^8] See [Care Plan Data Exchange]({{< relref "guides/care-plan-data-exchange" >}}). On 24 October a third party accessed Wiseman's MeLL+professional cloud. Wiseman said no personal-information leak was confirmed; voice-record AI and CareRepo were still down as of 4 November.[^9][^10] See [Wiseman]({{< relref "competitors/wiseman" >}}).
