## In three lines

- MHLW tabled five draft viewpoints for the FY2027 care fee revision, and the first per-service proposals; the viewpoints are not yet decided.[^1][^2][^3]
- As of 1 October 2026 the care information platform is usable in 3 cities, with start dates published for 1,558 municipalities (89%).[^4]
- SMS and Kanamic each added an AI function that turns spoken care into a draft record.[^5][^6]

## FY2027 revision

On 30 September 2026 the 268th meeting of the Social Security Council Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee ({{< ja "社会保障審議会介護給付費分科会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo kyūfuhi bunkakai" "Social Security Council, Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee" >}}) discussed five draft viewpoints, after 9 sessions and 3 stakeholder exchanges since April 2026: (1) prices, wages and staff pay; (2) service provision under population decline, including the new specific-area services ({{< ja "特定地域サービス" "tokutei chiiki sābisu" "specific-area services" >}}) created by the June 2026 Act amending the Social Welfare Act etc., and reviewing mountainous-area add-ons; (3) community-based integrated care, including LIFE and paid-home transparency; (4) workplaces and productivity — AI and other care technology, including in home-based services, plus task shift/share; (5) stability, including organising and simplifying the fee structure ({{< ja "報酬体系の整理・簡素化" "hōshū taikei no seiri, kanso-ka" "organising and simplifying the fee structure" >}}). About 40% of operators were in deficit in the FY2025 survey of care business management (overview survey).[^1] Not decided; a secondary source says they go into the final review report ({{< ja "審議報告" "shingi hōkoku" "review report" >}}) after members' opinions.[^2]

The 269th meeting (9 October 2026) opened directions on regular-visit/on-call home care and nursing ({{< ja "定期巡回・随時対応型訪問介護看護" "teiki junkai zuiji taiō-gata hōmon kaigo kango" "regular-visit and on-call home care and nursing" >}}) with night-time home-visit care ({{< ja "夜間対応型訪問介護" "yakan taiō-gata hōmon kaigo" "night-time home-visit care" >}}); small-scale multifunctional home care; nursing small-scale multifunctional home care; and dementia group homes. Night-time home-visit care is being abolished and merged into regular-visit/on-call care. Proposed: a type (III) fee evaluation for offices that secure a 24-hour call and consultation function and provide daytime support (p.13), and a possible review of the home-helper staffing standard when a new office cannot find enough users for unavoidable local reasons (p.16). Discussion, not decisions.[^3] See [the money, the workforce, and what 2027 and 2040 look like]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}).

## Care information platform

As of 1 October 2026 the care information platform ({{< ja "介護情報基盤" "kaigo jōhō kiban" "care information platform" >}}) is usable in 3 cities (Ōita City and Beppu City, Ōita Prefecture; Ibusuki City, Kagoshima Prefecture). Start dates are published for 1,558 municipalities (89%); 42 still scheduling; 138 undecided. A 15 September update (status as of 10 September) had 2 cities (Ōita, Beppu), 1,548 published, 58 scheduling and 133 undecided; Ibusuki became the third city on 1 October.[^4] See [Kaigo WEB Service and its API]({{< relref "guides/kaigo-web-service-api" >}}).

## AI voice-to-record

On 17 September 2026 SMS partnered with ENBASE Inc. Stand LM ({{< ja "スタンドLM" "sutando eru emu" "Stand LM" >}}) creates records from conversations during care; after a review screen, records go to Kaipoke with one tap. It starts with home-visit nursing stations (nursing record II), then home-visit care, care management and welfare-equipment rental. Stand LM launched in June 2025.[^5] On 24 September 2026 Kanamic launched an AI function that creates care records from speech on Buddycom (Science Arts, Inc.) and LINE WORKS Radjar (LINE WORKS Corp.); staff check, edit and save on a smartphone.[^6] See [Kaipoke]({{< relref "competitors/kaipoke" >}}) and [Kanamic]({{< relref "competitors/kanamic" >}}).

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: the draft viewpoints put AI in home-based services and fee-structure simplification side by side, so vendors should expect add-on and code-table changes at the FY2027 revision and design for configurable rules. Voice-to-record is becoming a standard feature at incumbents (Kaipoke via partner, Kanamic natively), so for PSP it is table stakes rather than a differentiator. With only 3 cities live but 89% of municipalities holding published dates, most real platform traffic will come later, which gives a new vendor time to build the API connection.
{{< /analysis >}}
