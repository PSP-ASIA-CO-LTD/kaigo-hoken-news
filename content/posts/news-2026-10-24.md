## Highlights

- **National accident reporting from around April 2027.** MHLW will build one care-site accident reporting system inside the care-service information publication system. A draft form is out but still being adjusted; first reports still go to the municipality within about five days.[^1]
- **Welfare-equipment and renovation data to the national database.** MHLW will consider requiring insurer systems to send specific welfare-equipment sales and housing-renovation payment records to the kokuho-ren even when payment is not delegated. Tentative start: April 2028.[^2]
- **First 437 Thai community nurse volunteers start in November 2026.** DHSS says the first licensed-professional group has finished training and will work alongside village health volunteers, sub-district hospitals, caregivers and local governments.[^3]

## 1. National accident reporting system (Vol.1548, 21 Oct 2026)

On 21 October 2026, {{< ja "介護保険最新情報" "kaigo hoken saishin jōhō" "LTC insurance 'latest information' notice" >}} Vol.1548 said MHLW will build a unified {{< ja "介護現場における事故情報報告システム" "kaigo genba ni okeru jiko jōhō hōkoku shisutemu" "care-site accident information reporting system" >}} as a subsystem of the {{< ja "介護サービス情報公表システム" "kaigo sābisu jōhō kōhyō shisutemu" "care-service information publication system" >}}, planned to start around April 2027. A draft reporting form is attached; the form is still being adjusted, but after go-live its items are expected to become the system's input fields. First reports still go to the municipality promptly, within about five days, covering at least items 1–6.[^1] See [the monthly cycle]({{< relref "guides/monthly-cycle-plan-to-payment" >}}) and [Who does what]({{< relref "guides/who-does-what" >}}).

## 2. Welfare-equipment and housing-renovation data (Vol.1547, 21 Oct 2026)

On 21 October 2026, Vol.1547 said {{< ja "特定福祉用具販売" "tokutei fukushi yōgu hanbai" "specific welfare-equipment sales" >}} and {{< ja "住宅改修" "jūtaku kaishū" "housing renovation" >}} payments often never reach the {{< ja "介護保険総合データベース" "kaigo hoken sōgō dētabēsu" "LTC insurance comprehensive database" >}} unless the insurer has delegated payment to the {{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}. MHLW plans to consider making it a required function for insurer systems to send those records to the kokuho-ren, without making payment-delegation itself mandatory. Tentative operation: April 2028, assumed for applications from then onward.[^2]

## 3. Thailand: first community nurse volunteers

On 22 October 2026 DHSS said the first 437 {{< th "อาสาพยาบาลชุมชน (อสพ.)" "asasamak phayaban chumchon" "community nurse volunteers" >}} from the licensed-professional group had finished training and were ready to start in November 2026, working with village health volunteers (อสม.), sub-district hospitals, caregivers and local governments on monitoring, observation and referral of patients and vulnerable groups. More than 6,000 selected trainees started in October; a demand heat map is being prepared for the other two groups, expected December 2026–January 2027.[^3] See [Who does what in Thai community care]({{< relref "/thailand/who-does-what-community-care" >}}).
