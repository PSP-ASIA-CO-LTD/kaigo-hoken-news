## In three lines

- From 1 February 2027, rental welfare equipment with a built-in communication function has clear coverage rules; the communication costs themselves are never covered.[^1]
- On 26 October the LTC Insurance Committee reopened the "certain income or more" 20% co-pay threshold and how financial income and assets should count, and proposed lighter certification reviews.[^2][^3]
- Wiseman stopped MeLL+ and related services after unauthorized access on 24 October; no personal-data leak was confirmed as of 26 October.[^5]

## 1. Connected welfare equipment (Vol.1549, 26 Oct 2026)

{{< ja "介護保険最新情報" "kaigo hoken saishin jōhō" "LTC insurance 'latest information' notice" >}} Vol.1549 revises how {{< ja "福祉用具" "fukushi yōgu" "welfare equipment" >}} with a {{< ja "通信機能" "tsūshin kinō" "communication function" >}} is treated, applying from 1 February 2027. Wheelchairs (self-propelled power-assist and standard electric types), electric assist units, {{< ja "特殊寝台" "tokushu shindai" "special care beds" >}} and dementia wandering-detection devices stay covered when the communication part sends maintenance, repair or usage information to the user, family or equipment adviser. Wandering detectors that send location to family or neighbours via a portable receiver are also covered. Items whose communication function reports a user's change in condition or illness are wholly outside insurance. Communication fees, receiver devices and network set-up are never covered. MHLW may interview makers of connected products rented more than 100 times a month on average about pricing that includes communication fees.[^1] See [the monthly cycle]({{< relref "guides/monthly-cycle-plan-to-payment" >}}).

## 2. Benefits and burden, and certification (137th meeting, 26 Oct 2026)

The {{< ja "社会保障審議会介護保険部会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo hoken bukai" "Social Security Council, LTC Insurance Committee" >}} asked how to revise the {{< ja "一定以上所得" "ittei ijō shotoku" "'certain income or more'" >}} threshold for 20% co-pay, and how to reflect {{< ja "金融所得" "kin'yū shotoku" "financial income" >}} that currently escapes because withholding tax ends without a tax return. These are discussion points, not decisions.[^2] On {{< ja "要介護認定" "yō-kaigo nintei" "care-need certification" >}}, MHLW proposed allowing simplified review boards when the previous certification lasted 6 months or more (now 12), and publishing each municipality's simplification rate yearly.[^3] See [the money, the workforce, and what 2027 and 2040 look like]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}).

The same day, an interim summary on checking savings for the {{< ja "補足給付" "hosoku kyūfu" "supplementary benefit for low-income residents" >}} found 198 insurers already use online bank inquiries, about 8 days faster on average, and restated that the {{< ja "介護情報基盤" "kaigo jōhō kiban" "care information platform" >}} is due for full operation from 1 April 2028.[^4] See [the Kaigo WEB Service API guide]({{< relref "guides/kaigo-web-service-api" >}}).

## 3. Wiseman: MeLL+ professional unauthorized access

Wiseman said a third party accessed the cloud environment running MeLL+professional on 24 October. It stopped MeLL+ services, the voice-record AI option, the SOIN link, the Care-Repo portal and second-line, revoked the misused credentials and reported to regulators including the Personal Information Protection Commission. No leak was confirmed; services restart once safety is confirmed.[^5] See [Wiseman]({{< relref "competitors/wiseman" >}}).
