## Highlights

- **Specified-area services.** On 6 November the 274th meeting tabled FY2027 drafts for {{< ja "特定地域サービス" "tokutei chiiki sābisu" "specified-area services" >}}: offices in depopulating areas designated by the prefecture, expected to pre-register with the municipality, with looser staffing rules and fees the municipality may set.[^1] Home help could opt for a {{< ja "包括報酬" "hōkatsu hōshū" "bundled (flat-rate) fee" >}}.[^2] These are proposals, not decisions.
- **Safety, BCP, harassment.** Home services would get accident-prevention duties and day services would get restraint-reduction rules. The reduction for having no BCP would be abolished, and customer harassment would be added to operating standards.[^4][^5][^6]
- **Care Plan Data Exchange.** Login trouble from about 09:00 JST on 2 November was reported fixed in a 6 November update.[^7]

## 1. The 274th meeting (6 Nov)

The bundled home-help fee would be built on the user's care level, with bands by service content and visit frequency. Night and early-morning add-ons may be folded in. Pay-per-visit billing would remain for extra emergency visits, help getting to hospital, and two-carer visits. Offices in these areas that are not subject to the same-building reduction would get new travel-cost add-ons for home help, home bathing, day care and care management. For home help, this applies only to offices that stay on pay-per-visit billing.[^2] The {{< ja "特別地域加算" "tokubetsu chiiki kasan" "special-area add-on" >}} would extend to day services. Care-management {{< ja "サテライト事業所" "sateraito jigyōsho" "satellite office" >}} would become a formal category with its own add-on, and need no separate files if records are kept electronically at the main office through a cloud system.[^3]

Home-service offices would need an accident-prevention guideline, a system for reporting and analysing accidents, regular training and a named officer. There would be a one-year transition, and a committee would not be compulsory. Day services would have to take {{< ja "身体的拘束" "shintaiteki kōsoku" "physical restraint" >}} measures, with a base-fee cut if they do not.[^4] The duty to write a BCP itself stays.[^5] See [the money and 2027]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}).

## Also this week

The 6 November help-desk update gave no cause for the login trouble.[^7] See [Care Plan Data Exchange]({{< relref "guides/care-plan-data-exchange" >}}). In Thailand, the {{< th "กรมสนับสนุนบริการสุขภาพ (สบส.)" "krom sanapsanun borikan sukkhaphap" "Department of Health Service Support (DHSS)" >}} said on 2 November that it had post-audited 206 elder-care homes as of 26 October. It aims to cover all 1,204 by 30 November 2026. Problems found included unlicensed extra beds, poor waste handling, and admitting psychiatric patients without assessment.[^8]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: a bundled monthly fee is a different claim type from per-visit billing. If it is adopted, billing software will need both fee tables for each office, switched by the municipal registration. The accident-prevention duty and the [national accident-reporting system]({{< relref "news-2026-10-24" >}}) planned for around April 2027 point to one incident record that feeds both. The cloud-records clause for satellite offices favours cloud-first care-management tools.
