## Highlights

- **08:00 on 13 January.** On 25 November MHLW confirmed 13 January 2027 as the start date for care-plan sending on the {{< ja "介護WEBサービス" "kaigo webu sābisu" "Kaigo WEB Service" >}}, with use scheduled from 08:00. Offices are asked to try one send and one receive with a usual partner between 13 and 19 January.[^1] See [Care Plan Data Exchange]({{< relref "guides/care-plan-data-exchange" >}}) and [Kaigo WEB Service]({{< relref "guides/kaigo-web-service-api" >}}).
- **Same system at both ends.** A plan can be received only on the system it was sent from. Once the web service is steady, the old exchange is due to close around March 2027. Login, history and downloads then stop.[^2]
- **Keep a screenshot.** Save the send/receive history. That screenshot is what an office shows, if asked, for the fiscal 2026 special rule of the {{< ja "介護職員等処遇改善加算" "kaigo shokuin tō shogū kaizen kasan" "pay improvement add-on" >}}. How the add-on treats the two systems is still to come.[^3]
- **Five municipalities usable.** The 24 November portal update (as of 19 November) lists five municipalities as usable. {{< ja "浜中町" "Hamanaka-chō" "Hamanaka Town, Hokkaido" >}} moves from a 20 November start date to usable. Published start dates: 1,507, from 1,509 in the 17 November update (as of 12 November). Scheduling 81, undecided 148.[^4]
- **Premium stages, no new rates.** Papers for the 139th {{< ja "介護保険部会" "kaigo hoken bukai" "Long-Term Care Insurance Subcommittee" >}} (26 November) ask it to look again at the standard {{< ja "第１号保険料" "dai-ichigō hokenryō" "Type 1 premium, for people aged 65 and over" >}} stages, including the higher-income multiplier. The bands and multipliers come later.[^5] If a household leaves "nobody pays municipal tax" and the stage jump outruns the rise in ability to pay, an insurer could cut the rise in the multiplier by half for one year, from fiscal 2028.[^6] These are proposals, not decisions. See [the money and 2027]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}).

## The fixed date

Registration is required even where the municipality has not started the {{< ja "介護情報基盤" "kaigo jōhō kiban" "care information platform" >}}.[^1] A 24 November video walks current users through registration, and still calls the move "planned for January 2027".[^7] The flyer puts valid licences above 60% of care-database offices at the end of October 2026.[^8] A YouTube briefing, with no signup, is on 7 December at 14:00.[^9] {{< ja "国保中央会" "kokuho chūō-kai" "National Health Insurance Central Association" >}} has added servers for the month-end rush.[^10]

## Also in the 139th papers

The benefits-and-charges paper leaves the 20% user charge open. It adds 2024 figures on spending and savings, and asks how to judge the {{< ja "一定以上所得" "ittei ijō shotoku" "income above a set level" >}} test.[^11]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: from 13 January a care manager on the web service and a provider still on the old client cannot exchange a plan. Software should show which system the partner is on, and keep the old export until around March. The screenshot rule makes the send/receive log an audit file for the pay add-on. The premium change is an insurer billing rule for fiscal 2028, not a new claim line.
