## Highlights

- **Margins slip.** The FY2026 {{< ja "介護事業経営実態調査" "kaigo jigyō keiei jittai chōsa" "survey on the economic conditions of care businesses" >}} puts the average {{< ja "収支差率" "shūshi sa-ritsu" "operating margin" >}} for FY2025 accounts at 4.3%, down from 4.7%. {{< ja "通所介護" "tsūsho kaigo" "day care" >}} fell from 6.2% to 1.4%, while home help rose from 9.6% to 11.3%.[^1] Loss-making offices: 38.3% overall, 45.7% in day care and 46.2% in {{< ja "居宅介護支援" "kyotaku kaigo shien" "care management" >}}.[^2]
- **Pay up 5.0%.** At offices with the {{< ja "介護職員等処遇改善加算" "kaigo shokuin-tō shogū kaizen kasan" "pay improvement add-on" >}}, care workers' average monthly pay (full-time, monthly-paid) rose from ¥332,000 in July 2025 to ¥348,690 in July 2026.[^3] Of the care-management and home-nursing offices that skipped the FY2025 wage subsidy, 20.2% gave as a reason that they had not joined the Care Plan Data Exchange or did not know a pledge to join was enough.[^4] See [Care Plan Data Exchange]({{< relref "guides/care-plan-data-exchange" >}}).
- **Rewarding software in home and day services.** For FY2027, MHLW proposed a new reward for home, day and care-management offices that adopt care-record software (input through to storage), the {{< ja "ケアプランデータ連携システム" "kea puran dēta renkei shisutemu" "Care Plan Data Exchange" >}}, AI document tools, or tablets for every visiting worker. They would also need a safety committee and ongoing work improvement.[^5] The time-study report would stop being required for type I of the {{< ja "生産性向上推進体制加算" "seisansei kōjō suishin taisei kasan" "productivity add-on" >}}.[^5] See [subsidies and software buying]({{< relref "guides/subsidies-and-software-buying" >}}).

## Also this week

- **Simpler pay add-on.** MHLW proposed moving the workplace-environment and training requirements out of the pay add-on into the {{< ja "サービス提供体制強化加算" "sābisu teikyō taisei kyōka kasan" "service-provision add-on" >}}.[^6] It also asked whether part of the pay add-on should move into base fees.[^7] These are proposals, not decisions.

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: this is the clearest sign yet that FY2027 fees will pay home and day services for going digital, not only facilities. The proposed definition matters for product design: records must flow from input to storage in one system, and visiting staff need mobile devices. With day-care margins near 1%, buyers will want software that earns the new reward and cuts paperwork for the pay add-on.
