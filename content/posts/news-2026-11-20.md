## Highlights

- **Sales records from every insurer.** For FY2027, MHLW proposed that every municipality, including those that do not delegate claims, send {{< ja "特定福祉用具販売" "tokutei fukushi yōgu hanbai" "specific welfare-equipment sales" >}} records to the {{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}. It would use that national picture before considering extra items in the rental-or-sale choice system.[^1] A product whose average rentals fall below 100 a month would keep its current price cap.[^2] See [Who does what]({{< relref "guides/who-does-what" >}}).
- **Checks every six months.** MHLW proposed monitoring at least every six months for rental items outside the choice system, and, after a transition period, a cap on how many users one welfare-equipment adviser handles.[^3] These are proposals, not decisions.
- **Screening moves into LIFE.** MHLW proposed abolishing the oral-and-nutrition screening add-on and scoring that check inside the {{< ja "科学的介護推進体制加算" "kagakuteki kaigo suishin taisei kasan" "scientific-care system add-on" >}}.[^4] It also proposed one new reward for rehabilitation, nutrition and oral care done together, in place of several rehab add-ons, and would require the shared notes to be written into the individual plan.[^5] See [LIFE]({{< relref "guides/life-scientific-care-data" >}}).

## Also this week

- **One home visit.** For the {{< ja "ターミナルケアマネジメント加算" "tāminaru kea manejimento kasan" "terminal care-management add-on" >}}, MHLW proposed replacing two visits timed to the day of death with at least one home visit, plus passing on what was found and doing the needed coordination.[^6] See [Who does what]({{< relref "guides/who-does-what" >}}).
- **Dates slip again.** The 17 November portal update (status as of 12 November) still lists 4 cities live. Municipalities with a published start date fell to 1,509, from 1,523. Scheduling rose to 83 and undecided to 145.[^7]
- **One grant, two connections.** The FY2026 care-data grant can cover platform connection support and Care Plan Data Exchange connection support bought together. Exchange support on its own does not qualify. The deadline is 12 March 2027, and the budget may close sooner.[^8]
- **Voice AI back.** On 17 November Wiseman restarted its voice-record AI. The Care-Repo portal is still stopped.[^9] See [Wiseman]({{< relref "competitors/wiseman" >}}). ZEST said meetings and training now sync into Care-wing, and care instructions stay attached if the visit is reassigned.[^10] See [Care-wing]({{< relref "competitors/care-wing" >}}).

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: insurer systems that never delegated equipment-sales claims would have to send those records, and rental software would stop resetting a cap once a product falls below 100 loans a month. Oral and nutrition screening would become a scientific-care submission, and the combined rehab reward pays only if the shared notes land in the individual plan. The grant pays for the January move only when platform connection and the care-plan link are bought together.
{{< /analysis >}}
