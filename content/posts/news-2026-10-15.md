## In three lines

- MHLW proposed day-care changes for FY2027: bath-only short visits in under-served areas, no transport reduction for sudden cancellations after staff arrive, abolishing the 12–14 h extension bands, and travel-cost fee categories in place of the same-building reduction. All are proposals, not decisions.[^1]
- For short stays, MHLW proposed aligning the long-stay reduction from day 31 with the day-61 rule (with a seasonal exception), a cost-based transport add-on, and taking the medical-management add-on out of the benefit cap.[^2][^3]
- Thailand ordered DHSS to inspect all 1,203 licensed elder-care homes within two months; on 12 Oct a Nonthaburi home's licences were revoked (news report).[^4][^5]

## FY2027 day care and short stay (270th meeting, 14 Oct 2026)

On 14 October 2026 the 270th meeting of the Social Security Council Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee ({{< ja "社会保障審議会介護給付費分科会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo kyūfuhi bunkakai" "Social Security Council, Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee" >}}) tabled discussion proposals for day care, day rehab and short stays. They are not decisions.[^1][^2][^3][^6] See [the money, the workforce, and what 2027 and 2040 look like]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}) and [the monthly cycle]({{< relref "guides/monthly-cycle-plan-to-payment" >}}).

Day care ({{< ja "通所介護" "tsūsho kaigo" "day care" >}}): bath-only short visits as an exception in {{< ja "中山間地域" "chūsankan chiiki" "mountainous and remote areas" >}}; a video home-bathroom check by care staff under a doctor's instruction for {{< ja "入浴介助加算" "nyūyoku kaijo kasan" "bathing-assistance add-on" >}} type II; sudden cancellation after pickup staff arrive no longer triggers {{< ja "送迎減算" "sōgei gensan" "transport reduction" >}}; abolish extension bands ニ (12–13 h) and ホ (13–14 h); replace {{< ja "同一建物減算" "dōitsu tatemono gensan" "same-building reduction" >}} with travel-cost fee categories.[^1] Day rehab ({{< ja "通所リハビリテーション" "tsūsho rihabiritēshon" "day rehabilitation" >}}): {{< ja "リハビリテーションマネジメント加算" "rihabiritēshon manejimento kasan" "rehab-management add-on" >}} would require activity and participation goals and ADL/IADL training at meetings; fold the {{< ja "生活行為向上リハビリテーション実施加算" "seikatsu kōi kōjō rihabiritēshon jisshi kasan" "daily-living rehab add-on" >}} plan into the main rehab plan; {{< ja "退院時共同指導加算" "taiin-ji kyōdō shidō kasan" "discharge joint-guidance add-on" >}} would also cover discharges from {{< ja "介護老人保健施設" "kaigo rōjin hoken shisetsu" "long-term care health facility" >}} and {{< ja "介護医療院" "kaigo iryō-in" "long-term care medical centre" >}}.[^6] Short-stay daily-life ({{< ja "短期入所生活介護" "tanki nyūsho seikatsu kaigo" "short-stay daily-life care" >}}): {{< ja "長期利用減算" "chōki riyō gensan" "long-stay reduction" >}} from day 31 set at the day-61 level, seasonal exemption possible; {{< ja "送迎加算" "sōgei kasan" "transport add-on" >}} follow actual costs.[^2] Short-stay medical ({{< ja "短期入所療養介護" "tanki nyūsho ryōyō kaigo" "short-stay medical care" >}}): {{< ja "総合医学管理加算" "sōgō igaku kanri kasan" "comprehensive medical-management add-on" >}} would no longer count toward {{< ja "区分支給限度基準額" "kubun shikyū gendo kijun-gaku" "category benefit limit" >}}.[^3]

## Thailand: nationwide inspection order

On 11 October 2026 a Thai government spokesperson said the Minister of Public Health had ordered DHSS (สบส.), its 12 regional centres and provincial health offices to inspect all 1,203 registered elder-care homes within two months, under the Health Establishment Act B.E. 2559 and the 2020 ministerial regulation. Penalties cited: unlicensed operation up to six months or 50,000 baht; psychiatric patients without a clinic licence under the Sanatorium Act B.E. 2541 up to five years or 100,000 baht.[^4]

On 12 October 2026 Thai PBS reported (secondary) that DHSS had revoked the business and operator licences of Baan Soem Suk, Soi Rewadee 22, Nonthaburi: 14 beds against nine licensed, and 59 psychiatric or drug-related psychiatric patients without a clinic licence.[^5] See [Licensing elderly-care businesses]({{< relref "/thailand/licensing-elderly-care-businesses" >}}).

Tako-San's view: the 270th proposals touch claim logic vendors must keep configurable — transport reductions, extension bands, same-building vs travel-cost categories, and what counts inside the benefit cap. If adopted for FY2027, hard-coded fee tables will break. On the Thai side, a two-month sweep of all 1,203 homes plus a public revocation shows licensing risk is becoming operational, not just paperwork; software that tracks licence status, bed counts against licence, and whether a home is treating psychiatric patients outside its licence would matter to both regulators and buyers.
