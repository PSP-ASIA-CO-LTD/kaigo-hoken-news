## In three lines

- MHLW will reopen the care-provider business-information database on 26 Oct 2026, with new deadlines for the backlog and three new floor-area fields.[^1]
- The 271st benefits subcommittee meets on 19 Oct 2026 on home-visit services and care management for the FY2027 revision.[^2]
- The care-plan data exchange system posted its 16 Oct seminar recording (including the January 2027 move to the Kaigo WEB Service), and M3 disclosed a data breach at its pharma-facing MR-kun service.[^3][^4]

## Business-information reporting restarts (Kaigo hoken saishin joho Vol.1546, 15 Oct 2026)

On 15 October 2026, Vol.1546 ({{< ja "介護保険最新情報" "kaigo hoken saishin jōhō" "LTC insurance 'latest information' notice" >}}) said that, under Article 115-44-2 of the LTC Insurance Act, care providers must report business information (revenue, costs and other items set by ministerial ordinance) to the prefectural governor ({{< ja "都道府県知事" "todōfuken chiji" "prefectural governor" >}}).[^1] See [Who does what]({{< relref "guides/who-does-what" >}}).

MHLW had suspended reception of second and later reports (fiscal years ending March 2025 onward) by a notice of 23 April 2025, to make system changes that reduce reporting burden. Those changes are done, and reception on the care-provider business-information database ({{< ja "介護サービス事業者経営情報データベースシステム" "kaigo sābisu jigyōsha keiei jōhō dētabēsu shisutemu" "care-provider business-information database system" >}}) restarts on Monday 26 October 2026.[^1] Deadlines: fiscal years ending March 2025 to February 2026, 31 December 2026; March to December 2026, 31 March 2027; January 2027 onward, within 3 months of year end (the normal rule).[^1] New report items: total floor area ({{< ja "延べ床面積" "nobe yuka menseki" "total floor area" >}}) for each care service, for medical services, and for disability welfare services. Reports should in principle cover only the care business; providers that cannot split care from non-care revenue or costs report the listed items, including the new floor-area items.[^1]

## Next up, 271st meeting on 19 Oct

On 19 October 2026 the 271st meeting of the Social Security Council Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee ({{< ja "社会保障審議会介護給付費分科会" "shakai hoshō shingikai kaigo kyūfuhi bunkakai" "Social Security Council, Long-Term Care Benefits Subcommittee" >}}) is a web meeting, 10:00–13:00 JST (08:00–11:00 Bangkok time), streamed on YouTube; materials to be posted by 08:00 JST that day.[^2] Agenda for the FY2027 fee revision: home-visit care ({{< ja "訪問介護" "hōmon kaigo" "home-visit care" >}}), home-visit bathing ({{< ja "訪問入浴介護" "hōmon nyūyoku kaigo" "home-visit bathing" >}}), home-visit nursing ({{< ja "訪問看護" "hōmon kango" "home-visit nursing" >}}), home-visit rehab ({{< ja "訪問リハビリテーション" "hōmon rihabiritēshon" "home-visit rehabilitation" >}}), home medical management guidance ({{< ja "居宅療養管理指導" "kyotaku ryōyō kanri shidō" "home medical management guidance" >}}), and care management ({{< ja "居宅介護支援" "kyotaku kaigo shien" "care management (care-plan services)" >}}).[^2] Preview only, no decisions; the next news post will cover the proposals. See [the money, the workforce, and what 2027 and 2040 look like]({{< relref "04-budget-and-outlook" >}}) and [the monthly cycle]({{< relref "guides/monthly-cycle-plan-to-payment" >}}).

## Also this week

Care-plan data exchange ({{< ja "ケアプランデータ連携システム" "kea puran dēta renkei shisutemu" "care-plan data exchange system" >}}): the system's support site posted (16 October 2026) the archive video of its 16 October seminar, where provider users shared tips on making the system stick after adoption; the seminar also explained the move to the Kaigo WEB Service ({{< ja "介護保険資格確認等WEBサービス" "kaigo hoken shikaku kakunin-tō WEB sābisu" "Kaigo WEB Service (LTC insurance eligibility-check web service)" >}}) planned for January 2027; a Q&A extract PDF was posted.[^3] See [Care Plan Data Exchange]({{< relref "guides/care-plan-data-exchange" >}}).

On 15 October 2026 M3, Inc. said it confirmed unauthorized access on 14 October to the system of MR-kun ({{< ja "MR君" "MR-kun" "MR-kun (M3's pharma sales-rep service)" >}}), its service for pharmaceutical companies; leaked data include names, workplaces and some email addresses of some physician members (under 10% of physician members) and of several hundred pharma-company staff; login passwords and m3 points were not included; no public posting or misuse confirmed so far; investigation continues.[^4] The notice names only MR-kun and does not mention M3's care businesses.[^4] See [The M3 group in care]({{< relref "competitors/m3-group" >}}).

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: the reopened database is a concrete feature request for care software: accounting exports must split revenue and costs by service, and now floor area by service, which is harder for operators that also run medical or disability lines. The 31 Dec 2026 backlog deadline lands just before the January 2027 Kaigo WEB Service move, so providers and vendors face a crowded year end. The M3 incident did not touch care software, but it is a reminder that Japanese buyers will look closely at a vendor's security and incident disclosure.
