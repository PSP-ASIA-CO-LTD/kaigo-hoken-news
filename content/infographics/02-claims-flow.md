<p>The 10th-of-the-month deadline is <strong>hard</strong>. Miss it and the claim waits another month. This is why most care software vendors emphasise easy record-to-claim linking: the fewer manual steps between "service delivered" and "claim ready", the less risk of missing the cut-off. For PSP Asia, any software you build or integrate needs to make that 10th-day deadline impossible to miss.</p>
