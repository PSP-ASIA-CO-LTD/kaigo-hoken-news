These guides cover the national systems and rules that decide whether a care-software product can sell in Japan. Unlike the dated posts, each guide is **updated in place**. The "Last updated" line at the top and the changelog at the bottom show what changed and when.

Every fact in a guide has a numbered footnote that points to a source we opened, and each guide ends with a References list. Opinion appears only in the "Tako-San's take" box.

Start with the LIFE guide, then read the Care Plan Data Exchange guide. For how the system works month to month, read [the monthly cycle from plan to payment](monthly-cycle-plan-to-payment/), [who does what](who-does-what/), and [subsidies and how offices buy software](subsidies-and-software-buying/). For what care software must build for the 2027 platform, read [Kaigo WEB Service and its API](kaigo-web-service-api/). For the government list that prefectures check before subsidising software, read [MHLW's care-software function survey](care-software-function-survey/). For how METI's IT subsidy compares with MHLW's care-technology subsidy, read [METI or MHLW?](meti-vs-mhlw-software-subsidies/). For paid homes and serviced housing for older people, and how they bill care insurance, read [Private elderly housing](private-pay-elderly-housing/). For the visa routes for foreign care workers, the language and work rules, and MHLW support for multilingual care software, read [Foreign care workers and multilingual software](foreign-care-workers-and-multilingual-software/). The competitor profiles are in the [Competitors](../competitors/) section.
