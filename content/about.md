**Kaigo Hoken News** is a research publication covering Japan's long-term care insurance system (介護保険, *kaigo hoken*) for international audiences.

## Publisher

[PSP Asia Co., Ltd.](https://pspasia.com) is a Thai company that builds caregiving management software. We are studying the Japanese care market to understand its regulatory structure, technology ecosystem, and operational practices.

## Purpose

These articles document what we learn as we research the Japanese care system. The content is meant to help other international care-technology companies, researchers, and policymakers understand how Japan funds and delivers long-term care services.

## Sources

All statistics and policy details are sourced from official Japanese government publications, primarily from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (厚生労働省). Links to source documents are provided throughout each article.

For questions or corrections, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/psp-asia-co-ltd/kaigo-hoken-news).
