*Last updated: 11 Oct 2026*

Caleo is an AI care-records and operations system for elderly-care homes, nursing homes, home care, day care, long-term care and rehabilitation services in Thailand.[^1] It says it was "built in 2026". It is in a "Founding Provider phase", onboarding its first customers on launch pricing.[^1][^2] Caleo is the only vendor in this section whose marketing is built around inspection by the Department of Health Service Support (DHSS), กรมสนับสนุนบริการสุขภาพ (Krom Sanapsanun Borikan Sukkhaphap, abbreviated สบส.).[^1][^3]

## Pricing

Caleo does not publish prices. It describes "simple, transparent per-bed pricing with no long lock-ins" and offers a 30-minute demo, after which it gives "an exact quote".[^1] Its offer to founding providers has five parts:[^1][^3]

- launch pricing
- no set-up fee
- free data migration
- on-site training
- an informal promise that "if your team doesn't feel lighter in the first 30 days, we can talk frankly"

Families use the LINE portal at no cost and do not install an app.[^1]

## Main features

From Caleo's English home page and Thai nursing-home page:[^1][^3]

- **AI care notes.** Carers speak or type a line at the bedside, and Caleo writes a structured, "person-centred" record. The carer approves every note.[^4] Caleo claims "up to 70% less time on paperwork" and voice entry "in 10 seconds".[^1][^3] These are vendor claims with no study cited.
- **Risk alerts.** AI watches trends across notes, medication and observations. It flags things like weight loss, low fluid intake and missed doses before they become incidents.
- **eMAR.** Electronic medication records with scan-to-confirm and live medication rounds. Missed and refused doses show immediately.
- **Rostering.** Shifts, staff assignment, warnings of uncovered shifts, and tracking of training and certifications.
- **Mobile carer app.** Voice logging, shifts and the day's tasks. It works on a weak signal and syncs later.
- **LINE Family Portal.** Meals, mood, activities and fluid intake shown to families through LINE.
- **Inspection readiness.** "Every entry mapped to elder-care standards". The system generates evidence packs, audits and reports "for inspection in a few clicks".
- **Migration.** Residents, care plans, medication records and rotas moved from paper, Excel, LINE or another system, "usually within a few days".

## Target customers

Caleo names six settings: care homes, nursing homes, home care, day care, long-term care and rehabilitation.[^1] ZestMate's page on Caleo describes the target more narrowly:[^4]

- small and mid-sized care centres and nursing homes
- home-care providers
- day care, rehabilitation and long-term care
- "owners who want to pass DHSS inspection without drowning in paper"

The Thai nursing-home page opens with a scenario: a night nurse copying notes for 30 residents the night before a DHSS inspection.[^3] That shows the size of home Caleo has in mind.

## Company

- Caleo's about page names its founder as Pongsiri Trivittayasil. It says he has "over a decade in digital marketing, automation, and software development". It describes a team based in Thailand and a product "not translated from a foreign product".[^2]
- ZestMate Solution, which describes itself as a Zoho consultancy, says Caleo is "developed and maintained by the same Thai team as ZestMate". It says Caleo is a separate product with its own website, sign-up and support, and that it is not a Zoho product.[^4]
- Neither site names the legal entity that sells Caleo. We could not check the Department of Business Development (DBD) registry: DBD DataWarehouse requires a sign-in, and we found no DBD-derived record that names Caleo. The founding date and registered capital are therefore unknown.

## Reporting and integration: DHSS, NHSO and 3C

- **DHSS (สบส.).** This is Caleo's main pitch. Its pages say the following:[^1][^3][^4]
  - it is "aligned to สบส. standards"
  - "every record maps to the relevant criteria"
  - it can export evidence for a DHSS inspection "in a few clicks"
  - it keeps an audit log of every edit

  The English home page speaks of "MoPH" (Ministry of Public Health) standards and inspections. The about page and the Thai pages name สบส. specifically.[^1][^2][^3] The pages do not say which DHSS standards or forms are covered. Licensed homes are inspected on, among other things, a client register in a set form, a needs reassessment every three months and a staff register (see [Licensing elderly-care businesses]({{< relref "/thailand/licensing-elderly-care-businesses" >}})). Caleo does not show any sample output, so we could not check the claim.
- **NHSO LTC programme and 3C.** Not mentioned.
- **Other systems.** No API or integration partners are mentioned.

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: Caleo is very new, has no published customers or prices, and its company is not yet identifiable. It is still worth watching, because it has picked the right message. In Thailand the legal duty on a private care home is to keep records ready for DHSS inspection, not to send claims. Caleo is the first vendor we found that sells directly on that duty. If it can show real DHSS evidence packs, it sets the bar that CloudNurse, HexaHealth and others will be measured against. Any vendor that wants to sell to licensed homes will need a clear, checkable answer to "show me the DHSS register and reassessment forms". The other point is its pedigree. It comes from a CRM consultancy, which suggests strength in quick set-up and sales, but its clinical depth is not yet shown. Check its status again in six months. Start-ups at the "founding provider" stage often change or disappear.
