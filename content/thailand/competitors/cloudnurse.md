*Last updated: 11 Oct 2026*

CloudNurse (คลาวด์เนิร์ส, Khlaodnoet) is a cloud system for running private elderly-care homes. It covers care records, medication, incidents, supplies, family reports and billing.[^1][^2] It calls itself "Thailand's first digital elderly care platform" and "the first management system built for Thai nursing homes".[^1] Those are the vendor's own descriptions. The press coverage of its launch in November 2024 names the company behind it as TH Healthtech Co., Ltd., บริษัท ทีเอช เฮลท์เทค จำกัด (Borisat Thi-Et Henthek Chamkat).[^3]

## Pricing

CloudNurse publishes two plans:[^6]

| Plan | Price | Limits and extras |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 300 baht per resident per month | Up to 50 residents. Includes e-charting, eMAR, incident reports, automatic family reports, supply tracking and LINE notifications. Online staff training, LINE support, free data import, 99% SLA. |
| Enterprise | "Contact us" | Everything in Basic, plus an analytics dashboard, staff shift scheduling, billing and invoicing, API integration, unlimited residents, multiple branches, a dedicated account manager, free on-site training, priority data import and a 99.9% SLA. |

There is a 14-day free trial and no credit card is needed.[^6] The pricing page's own calculator shows 30 residents costing 9,000 baht a month.[^6] That is simply 30 × 300. In November 2024, the CEO told Positioning magazine that the subscription would be 350 baht per person per month.[^3] So the published price has come down since launch.

**Inconsistency on the site.** The home page lists "Care-Center Finance" (quotations, invoices, tax invoices, receipts, contracts) as a main feature.[^1] The pricing table puts "Billing & invoicing" in Enterprise only.[^6] It is not clear whether Basic customers get any billing tools.

## Main features

From the vendor's own pages:[^1][^2][^6]

- **e-Charting.** Each resident's condition and care history.
- **Medication records (eMAR).** Scheduled medication with an administration history.
- **AI assistant.** Turns staff speech and photos into structured records and reports, and writes AI-assisted family reports.
- **Family reports through LINE.** The system gathers each day's care data and sends families a report through LINE automatically.
- **Supplies.** Tracks items deposited by families and supplied by the home, warns when stock runs low, and asks families to top up through LINE.
- **Incident reports, and a dashboard** of occupancy, care status and resource use.
- **Care-centre finance.** Quotations, invoices, tax invoices, receipts, contracts and service charges. The pricing table places billing in Enterprise only (see above).

## Target customers

The product is aimed at private elderly-care homes and nursing homes.[^1][^2] Testimonials on the site come from Roserin Homecare, MyLuck Nursing Home and AYU-x. The site gives no locations or sizes for these customers.[^1] The "for nursing homes" page quotes "Asia Nursing Home, Bangkok" as saying CloudNurse cut paperwork time by more than 80%.[^2] That is a customer statement published by the vendor.

The CEO said in 2024 that marketing is easy because care homes belong to an association: "we go to the association, and that's it".[^3] He did not name the association.

## Scale: what the vendor has said

| Date | Source | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| 13 Nov 2024 | CEO, in Positioning magazine[^3] | 3 customer homes with 100 beds in total. Talks under way with 20 homes. Target of 100 homes in 2025. Founded with an investment of US$100,000. |
| Undated (seen 11 Oct 2026) | CloudNurse website[^2] | "35+" care homes, "300+" resident accounts, "150+" users |

The 100-home target for 2025 has not been reached on the vendor's own figure ("35+"). The two dates are almost two years apart, and the website figure is undated.

**Market claims to treat with care.** In the same 2024 interview, the CEO gave a market estimate of about 3,000 elderly-care establishments and 60,000 beds in Thailand. He put the software market for care-home back offices at about 2,000 million baht, and said CloudNurse had "no competitors".[^3] These are the founder's estimates, not official figures. DHSS counted 1,203 licensed homes in October 2026 (see [Licensing elderly-care businesses]({{< relref "/thailand/licensing-elderly-care-businesses" >}})). The competitors profiled in this section show that the "no competitors" claim no longer holds.

## Company

- **Two company names.** Press coverage in November 2024 names the operator as TH Healthtech Co., Ltd., with Thanasak Hoontrakul as co-founder and CEO.[^3] In September 2026, the National Innovation Agency (NIA) listed "TH HEALTHTECH CO., LTD., developer of 'CloudNurse'" among eight Thai firms at its Innovation Thailand Pavilion at Medical Fair Asia 2026 in Singapore.[^4] The CloudNurse website footer and pricing pages say "CloudNurse Co., Ltd.".[^6] We found no company of that name in the records we could reach. Treat the footer name as unverified until checked in DBD.
- **Registration.** Creden Data shows TH Healthtech Co., Ltd., juristic number 0105564124668.[^5] It gives these details:[^5]
  - registered 20 August 2021, status "still operating"
  - registered capital 10,500,000 baht
  - shareholders' equity about 1.43 million baht in the latest filing
  - classed as a "micro" business by revenue
  - financial statements filed for 2021–2025
  - address: Phahonyothin Road, Phaya Thai, Bangkok

  Creden republishes data from the Department of Business Development (DBD), กรมพัฒนาธุรกิจการค้า (Krom Phatthana Thurakit Kan Kha). We could not open DBD DataWarehouse directly: its search requires a sign-in.

## Reporting and integration: DHSS, NHSO and 3C

- **DHSS (สบส.).** None of the CloudNurse pages we opened mentions DHSS standards or inspections.[^1][^2][^6] Licensed homes must keep a client register, a three-monthly reassessment and a staff register (see [Licensing elderly-care businesses]({{< relref "/thailand/licensing-elderly-care-businesses" >}})). CloudNurse does not say whether its records map to these.
- **NHSO LTC programme and 3C.** Not mentioned. CloudNurse targets fee-paying private homes, which are outside the NHSO community LTC fund.
- **Other systems.** "API integration" is listed for Enterprise.[^6] We found no published API documentation and no named integration partners.
- **Data protection.** The privacy notice refers to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). It describes data-subject rights and "appropriate security measures" in general terms.[^1]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: CloudNurse is the closest thing Thailand has to a Japanese-style care-records start-up. It publishes a per-resident price and sells mainly on cutting paperwork and on LINE reports to families. Its numbers show how slowly this market moves: three homes at launch, "35+" almost two years later, against a target of 100. For any care-software vendor in Thailand, the 300 baht per resident per month price point is now public and will be quoted back in negotiations. The visible gap is regulation. Its pages say nothing about the DHSS records a licensed home is inspected on, nothing about NHSO or 3C, and they disagree with each other about which plan includes billing. Caleo is already marketing itself on DHSS inspection readiness. That is likely where competition in private homes will be fought. The mismatch between the company names (TH Healthtech in the press and at NIA, "CloudNurse Co., Ltd." on the website) should be resolved with DBD before anyone relies on it in a contract or comparison.
