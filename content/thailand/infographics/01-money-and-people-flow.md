<p>The picture is three separate tracks, and public money does not follow an older person into a private care home. NHSO money (10,442 baht a year per person) goes to local governments and service units, and only against an approved individual care plan. Private homes are paid by residents and families. Their regulator, DHSS, licenses and inspects them but pays them nothing. DOP runs its own centres and caregivers on a third track. For any care-software vendor, that means three kinds of buyer with three different reasons to buy. Local bodies need the assessment, care-plan and visit records the NHSO money depends on. Private homes need records that are ready for DHSS inspection. State bodies already have free government tools. The flows missing from this map, above all who writes and approves the care plan, are the first questions to put to the NHSO and the Department of Health.</p>

## What this diagram cannot show yet

- **Who writes the care plan.** Our Thai guides do not yet source a care-manager role (who writes the plan, how many there are, or how they are paid), so there is no care-manager box.
- **Who approves the individual care plan.** "Approved" is shown as an attribute of the plan; there is no approval node or arrow.
- **Who funds the 2025 caregiver stimulus project.** Local governments hire the caregivers and sign the agreements, but the source of the money behind those wages is not shown.
- **How the old-age allowance budget reaches local governments.** There is no national-budget-to-LAO arrow, and the Ministry of Finance role is not drawn.
- **What DOP centre residents pay, and the centres' budget.** There is no money arrow into the welfare centres.
- **Who pays the DOP caregivers' 10,000 baht stipend.** The Bangkok Post figure is in the DOP caregivers panel, not drawn as a money arrow.
- **Private-home prices.** No official national data was found, so the family-to-home arrow has no amount.
- **How the NHSO lump sum is spent downstream.** There is no arrow from that money to caregiver pay or equipment.
- **Who trains community caregivers on the 70-hour course.** There is no schools-to-community-caregiver arrow.
- **Which bodies count as service units.** The node is kept generic.
- **Local governments' own contributions** to the local health security fund, and payment timing, are not shown.
