<p>Follow one care plan through the cycle and it is entered at least twice: once in the Department of Health's 3C program, then again, after a paper approval, by the local government in the NHSO's programme, where the money is paid. The 2021 study our guides cite names this double entry as a burden on care managers. The two agencies agreed to link their data in 2018, and the link is now reported, in a news story, for 2027 as "3C PLUS". The caregiver, who gives the daily care, has no national record of their own that our guides could find: they work from a printed plan, and the care manager enters their work. The side tools, Blue Book for screening, Smart อสม. for volunteers and Thai COC for hospital referrals, each hold part of the same person's story, with no published link to the two core systems. For anyone designing care records here, the useful questions are where a record is first entered and how many times it is entered again, more than which features any one system has.</p>

## What this diagram cannot show yet

- **The caregiver's own visit log.** Our guides found no national form or app for it in the NHSO scheme, so it is drawn as a gap.
- **The format of the caregiver's monthly report** to the local government under the 2025 stimulus project. The guideline asks for the report but does not say how it is sent.
- **Where caregiver pay is recorded** under the first two pay routes (the LTC fund through a service unit, and local care volunteers). Only the stimulus route is recorded in ltcnew.
- **A national calendar.** The August deadline and 15-day payment rule come from one municipal fund's 2016 guideline. The NHSO programme showed one data-entry window (1 to 15 September 2026), but no full calendar was found.
- **Current definitions of the four care groups and of TAI.** The programme asks for both on every plan; the fullest description found is from 2016.
- **How 3C works inside**, such as how accounts are approved. The Department of Health's 3C report could not be reopened.
- **Whether Blue Book, Smart อสม. or Thai COC send data to 3C or ltcnew.** None is published.
- **Who in practice types the plans into ltcnew.** The NHSO manual gives the task to the local government; a fact sheet says the care manager enters each person's details for the claim. Some areas train local-government staff to take the paperwork side.
