<p>The map splits in half. Every product with a published price sells to private homes or clinics. Every tool on the community side is a state system with no price published, or, for Thai COC in 2018, no charge. No private product states a link to the NHSO programme or 3C; on the state side, only 3C draws on the NHSO programme, and no link back was found. Only Caleo mentions DHSS inspection. All six care and clinic products mention LINE. The empty cells, no priced product for community care and no private product linked to the state systems, show where this market has not been tested yet; they are a description of the map, not a forecast. Prices are not comparable across rows either: CloudNurse charges per resident per month, while APSX and MCS sell yearly packages by branch and user. NEC's trial is the only foreign attempt we found, and nothing public followed after 2023. Check the cells marked "not published" with each vendor before relying on them.</p>

## What this map cannot show yet

- **NEC Mimamori's links.** NEC is not in our feature matrix, so its box has no badges.
- **Prices that can be compared.** HexaHealth's tiers contradict each other and do not say whether they are monthly; Caleo and the MCS elderly-care edition publish no price. The map shows each pricing model, not a ranking.
- **Market share or customer counts on one scale.** No source we found gives market share, and customer counts are the vendors' own, undated figures, so box size means nothing.
- **Whether the state tools are free.** Only Thai COC states no charge, and only for 2018. For 3C, the NHSO LTC programme, Blue Book and Smart อสม. no price is published.
- **Nirun's centre system and its community app separately.** The two share one box; only the community app is reported (in the press) to run through LINE.
- **Hospital information systems and caregiver-matching apps.** Our competitor section does not cover them yet.
