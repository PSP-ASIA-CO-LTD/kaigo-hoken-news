<p>Walk one person through the year and the shape of the scheme is clear: almost everything that decides whether care happens and is paid for sits in the first four steps, a screening score, a registration, a written plan and a paper approval, and the money follows the approved plan rather than the visits. After that, the twelve months of actual care leave the thinnest trail. The caregiver works from a printed plan, and we found no national record of their visits, while the amount spent on any one person is left to the local subcommittee. That is why this is a walk-through, not a calculator: the public rules fix the entry and the year-end record, but not the cost of one person's care in between.</p>

## What this walk-through cannot show yet

- **How much of the 10,442 baht is spent on one person.** We found no current rule that splits it by care group.
- **The caregiver's own visit log.** No national form or app was found in the NHSO scheme.
- **Current official care-group definitions and the TAI classification.** The fullest description we found is a 2016 annex.
- **A national calendar.** The July and August dates come from one fund's 2016 guideline.
