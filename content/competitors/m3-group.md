*Last updated: 9 Oct 2026*

M3, Inc. ({{< ja "エムスリー株式会社" "emu surī kabushiki-gaisha" "M3, Inc." >}}, TSE Prime 2413) runs "m3.com", a website for medical professionals. M3 says more than 350,000 doctors in Japan, over 90% of the country's physicians, are registered.[^1] Since 2022 it has bought three companies that sell to care providers: **Logic** (Care-wing records software, 2022), **Elan** (the "CS set" of clothing and daily supplies for hospital patients and care residents, 2024) and **Wiseman** (care and medical software, 2026).[^2][^3][^1] M3's listed subsidiary **CUC** also runs home-visit nursing, hospices and housing for people who need care.[^4]

This page sets out what is on public record. Every figure comes from M3's filings and investor documents, from TDnet, or from the companies' own releases. Statements of strategy are M3's own words, in our translation, and are labelled as such. For the products, see our profiles of [Wiseman]({{< relref "wiseman" >}}) and [Care-wing]({{< relref "care-wing" >}}).

## Deal terms on public record

### Wiseman (2026)

M3's board approved the purchase on **5 June 2026**, and the share-transfer agreement was signed the same day.[^1] The terms M3 disclosed are:[^1][^10]

- **What M3 buys.** All of Wiseman's shares, which will be **242 shares** after Wiseman buys back shares from its other holders before the transfer. The sellers are Wiseman's president, Sōichirō Minamidate, and MMK LLC, an asset-management company in which he holds 99.96%.[^1]
- **Price.** Not disclosed, "at the request of the counterparties".[^1] M3 obtained a valuation from KPMG FAS, which used a discounted-cash-flow method, and says the price is within the valuation range.[^1]
- **Funding.** Paid entirely from cash on hand.[^10]
- **Expected effect.** M3 expects the deal to raise profit even after amortising intangible assets, with a positive effect on earnings per share.[^10] Its notice says the effect on consolidated results was still being examined.[^1]
- **Transfer date.** 1 July 2026, marked "scheduled".[^1]

Wiseman's results for its last three years, as given in M3's notice:[^1]

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 11,354 | 457 | 3,196 | 20,070 |

M3's deck adds a forecast for the year to June 2026 of ¥13,980 million in sales and ¥1,586 million in operating profit.[^10]

**Did the deal close on 1 July 2026?** The sources point to yes, but no document we opened confirms the exact day:

- M3's results for April–June 2026 (published 6 August 2026) do not mention Wiseman. That quarter ended on 30 June, before the scheduled transfer.[^6]
- M3's company presentation of 6 August 2026 has a slide headed "Making Wiseman a subsidiary" (ワイズマンの子会社化).[^11]
- M3's Report for Investors 2026 (dated 30 September 2026) says: "Announced in June 2026, Wiseman consolidated as a subsidiary from July." It also refers to "the July 2026 acquisition of Wiseman", and the CEO's message names Wiseman as having joined the group this year.[^4]
- We found no separate completion notice from M3, and Wiseman's news page has no item on the transfer after 5 June 2026.[^12]

So M3's own report says the deal took effect in July 2026, but none of the documents we opened gives the day. M3's next quarterly results should show Wiseman's first consolidated figures.

### Elan (2024)

M3's board decided on **19 September 2024** to buy Elan shares by tender offer and to sign a capital and business alliance with Elan. The offer ran from 20 September to **21 October 2024**.[^3][^13]

- **Price.** ¥1,040 per share.[^3]
- **Cap and result.** M3 capped the offer at 33,329,453 shares. Holders tendered 45,172,994 shares, so M3 bought on a pro-rata basis and acquired 33,329,490 shares, or **55.00%** of the voting rights.[^3]
- **Cost.** ¥34,662 million, paid in cash.[^13]
- **Date.** Elan became a consolidated subsidiary on 21 October 2024, the last day of the offer; settlement began on 28 October 2024.[^3]
- **Governance.** Elan remains listed. Under the alliance, M3 may nominate two non-executive directors, and Elan's independent outside directors and M3's nominees together must make up at least half of Elan's board. M3 has set up a special committee to protect Elan's minority shareholders in group transactions.[^4]

### Logic (2022)

M3 announced on **1 March 2022** that Logic would become a subsidiary on **1 April 2022**.[^2] Logic's own release confirms that it joined on that date.[^14] Neither release gives a price. M3's June 2024 annual securities report lists its voting stake as 100.0%.[^5]

## What M3 says its strategy is

The statements below are **M3's own words**, in our translation. They are aims, not results.

- **Why Wiseman.** M3 says that by combining "Wiseman's strong customer base and deep knowledge of the care and welfare field" with M3's technology, it "will strongly promote DX in care settings". It aims to improve productivity and care quality and to "realise medical–care coordination that seamlessly connects medical institutions and care facilities".[^1]
- **Four synergies.** M3's deck lists:[^10]
  1. using M3's AI to add value to Wiseman products, "extending the AI success cases in Logic's Care-wing"
  2. linking M3's cloud electronic health record "M3 Digikar" ({{< ja "エムスリーデジカル" "emu surī dejikaru" "M3 Digikar" >}}) with Wiseman's medical–care link system "MeLL+" to share information between medical institutions and care facilities
  3. developing new services in care with the group's know-how
  4. cross-selling the group's care services ("Elan, Logic") to Wiseman's "strong customer base in the care market", and lifting Wiseman's sales with M3's web marketing
- **Physician network and records.** M3 puts the link-up in numbers: about **1,200** regional-coordination installations of MeLL+ (end-October 2025) alongside about **9,600** Digikar installations (end-March 2026).[^10] By the August 2026 results, M3 gave Digikar about **10,100** cumulative installations.[^11]
- **Data.** M3 describes three things it offers the care sector: (A) technology, meaning AI-driven automation of operations; (B) a platform for optimal staffing and sharing across facilities; and (C) "data evidence", meaning data-based standardisation of care.[^10]
- **Market size and target.** M3 says care costs will rise from about ¥11 trillion in 2020 to about ¥28 trillion in 2040, citing MHLW data. It aims to help cut about 10% (about ¥3 trillion) of future care costs through DX. It treats 10% of that saving (about ¥300 billion) as the size of a "DX-support market", and aims for a **30% share** of it together with Wiseman.[^10]
- **Wiseman's role.** "Wiseman is positioned as the platform for realising the 'one person, one yen' mission in the care field as well."[^11] M3's mission is to use technology "creatively to increase by even one the number of people who live long, healthy and enjoyable lives, and to reduce unnecessary medical costs by even one yen".[^4]
- **Where care fits.** The CEO's message describes M3 covering prevention, then treatment, and then "staying with people who still need care and other support to the very end". It names Wiseman as the newest part of that care business.[^4]
- **Why Elan.** M3 says it bought Elan to combine "our physician member base and the group's healthcare services" with "the business and customer base of the CS set".[^13] It plans mutual customer referrals and wants to use the CS set's direct contact with patients and residents to offer other group services.[^4]
- **Why Logic.** M3 called Care-wing a "foothold" ({{< ja "足掛かり" "ashigakari" "foothold" >}}) for digitising home-visit care and nursing. It planned to strengthen Care-wing's sales with the group's network and web marketing.[^2]

## Open questions

- **Wiseman's closing date and price.** M3 says it consolidated Wiseman from July 2026, but the documents we opened do not give the exact day. The price is undisclosed. The goodwill M3 books may show the scale of the deal when its next results are published.[^1][^4]
- **Where Wiseman sits in M3's segments.** The investor report lists "care-site DX" under Medical Platform with a note on Wiseman.[^4] The first segment figures that include Wiseman have not yet been published.
- **Care-wing and Wiseman.** Both sell to home-visit care offices.[^15][^8] M3 has not said how the two will be positioned against each other. Care-wing also links to billing software from M3's competitors.[^15]
- **The MeLL+–Digikar link.** M3 has named it as a synergy[^10] but has not published a timetable or technical design in the sources we opened.
- **Data.** M3 speaks of "data evidence" and data-based standardisation of care.[^10] It has not said what data from care offices would be used, on what legal basis, or with what consent.
- **The 30% target.** The ¥300 billion "DX-support market" is M3's own construct (10% of a hoped-for ¥3 trillion saving).[^10] It is not an external market measure.
- **Elan.** M3 holds 55.0% and Elan stays listed.[^3][^4] M3 has not said whether that will change.
- **Pricing changes.** Wiseman told customers that services, support and contract terms would not change significantly.[^12] Whether Wiseman's five-year licence model will move towards M3's SaaS-style products is not stated.

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: M3 is assembling an unusual mix: a care-software base (Wiseman, Care-wing), a supplies business inside hospitals and facilities (Elan), care and nursing operations of its own (CUC), and a direct channel to most of Japan's doctors through m3.com and Digikar. The care software is small next to the group: on M3's own numbers, Wiseman's sales are a few per cent of M3's revenue. But M3's stated aim is a medical–care link, not care software for its own sake.

For a newcomer such as PSP, the overlap is widest in facilities, where M3 claims Wiseman's strongest shares, and in home-visit care, where Care-wing sells. Day services show the thinnest M3 presence in the sources we opened: no share claim and no group product beyond Wiseman's. The medical–care link is where M3 would be hardest to match, because no newcomer has a physician network. A newcomer can partner on that link but cannot copy it. Everything M3 says about synergies, the 30% target and AI savings is a plan or a model. I would treat it as intent until M3 reports results that include Wiseman.
{{< /analysis >}}
