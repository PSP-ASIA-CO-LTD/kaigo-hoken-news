*Last updated: 9 Oct 2026*

Care-wing ({{< ja "Care-wing 介護の翼" "kea uingu kaigo no tsubasa" "Care-wing, 'wings of care'" >}}) is a records and office-management system for home-visit care and home-visit nursing offices. Staff touch a smartphone to an IC tag at the user's home to confirm the day's tasks and create the care record without typing.[^1][^2] It is a **records** system. Care-wing does not make insurance claims itself; it passes actuals to linked billing software.[^3] The vendor is Logic Inc. ({{< ja "株式会社ロジック" "kabushiki-gaisha rojikku" "Logic Inc." >}}) of Kanazawa, Ishikawa Prefecture. Logic has been a subsidiary of M3, Inc. since **1 April 2022**.[^4][^5] For the wider group, see our page on [the M3 group in care](../m3-group/).

## Pricing

Logic does not publish a price list on the pages we opened. Its FAQ describes Care-wing as an ASP service charged as a system-use fee. With monthly payment, the first contract is for three years, after which it renews automatically each month. The initial cost is the IC tags. Offices supply their own smartphones or rent them from Logic.[^3] Logic offers a rental plan for dedicated smartphones, with the data charge included, called "Carepho" ({{< ja "ケアホ" "keaho" "Carepho" >}}).[^3]

On its page for serviced housing and paid homes, Logic says Care-wing is a **flat monthly fee** that does not change as the number of services or users grows.[^12] MHLW's October 2025 care-software function survey lists Care-wing's sales form as monthly payment.[^13]

M3's August 2026 results deck includes a modelled example. It says an average-sized office could save about ¥168,000 a month in staff time, against a "monthly system fee of ¥10,000–20,000". M3 notes that this is an estimate for an average-sized office based on case studies and its own research, assuming an hourly wage of ¥1,500.[^7] It is M3's illustration, not a price list.

## Main features

- **IC tag and smartphone records.** A helper touches the phone to the user's IC tag. The phone shows the day's tasks, and date, time, service type, staff and user are filled in automatically. Staff enter only special notes, vital signs and the user's condition.[^3] Logic says this works without IDs or passwords on the phone, and that the tag gives evidence of each visit.[^3] Logic holds patents on the method.[^1][^2]
- **Voice input.** Special notes can be entered by voice.[^3][^8]
- **Real-time view for managers.** What staff record appears at once on the management screen for the service coordinator ({{< ja "サービス提供責任者" "sābisu teikyō sekininsha" "service coordinator" >}}) and the next visitor.[^3]
- **Instructions for the specified-office add-on.** An instruction and report function, including bulk instructions and templates, is built in. Logic presents it as meeting the "instruction and confirmation" requirement of the specified-office add-on ({{< ja "特定事業所加算" "tokutei jigyōsho kasan" "specified-office add-on" >}}).[^14]
- **Shifts and wages.** Shift management linked to the service-use table, and automatic wage calculation from actuals, including adjustments for the treatment-improvement add-on.[^3]
- **Forms.** Care plans, nursing plans and nursing reports, monthly calendars, actuals lists and service records can be printed. Records are kept for at least five years.[^3]
- **Security.** Only registered phones can connect. Data is not stored on the phone, and screenshots can be restricted.[^3]

## Links to billing software

Logic says offices can keep their current billing software. Care-wing records flow into it, so the office can go from record to claim in one chain.[^15] The link page shows nine billing products as logos. They include **Honobono NEXT**, **CAREKARTE**, **Flowers NEXT**, **Quick Care 2** ({{< ja "Quickけあ2" "kuikku kea tsū" "Quick Care 2" >}}), **Kaishū Family** ({{< ja "介舟ファミリー" "kaishū famirī" "Kaishū Family" >}}) and **Smart Progress**, whose logo carries the line "Powered by wiseman".[^15]

Logic's FAQ says Care-wing "on its own cannot make long-term care insurance claims". Approved actuals appear on the service-provision table, and a linked insurance package makes the claim.[^3]

ZEST, the rostering and routing tool, lists Care-wing among the systems it links with.[^16]

## Target customers

Logic's FAQ says Care-wing covers home-visit care and home-visit nursing under long-term care insurance, the comprehensive community programme, and several disability services (home care, severe-disability home visits, accompanied outings, behavioural support). Offices for mobility support, care taxis, home-visit bathing and home-visit rehabilitation also use it.[^3] The Care-wing site has separate pages for serviced housing and paid homes ({{< ja "サ高住・有料老人ホーム" "sa-kō-jū, yūryō rōjin hōmu" "serviced housing for older people and paid homes" >}}) and for 24-hour regular-visit services. Both pages stress records for self-pay and in-house services as well as insurance services.[^12][^9]

## Scale and share: what Logic and M3 claim

All figures here are the vendor's or its owner's own:

- **Current claim.** More than **3,000** care offices, more than **55,000** helpers and more than **191,000** users, based on May 2025 usage (Logic).[^1]
- **At acquisition.** M3's March 2022 release said Care-wing was in use at about **2,400** offices. It also estimated that 85–90% of care and nursing offices still kept care records on paper.[^4]
- **Time savings.** M3's June 2026 Wiseman deck shows Care-wing cutting monthly time on records, monitoring, payroll, add-on instructions, reconciliation and shift work by "about 90%" after its AI functions were added. M3 says this is a modelled estimate for an average-sized office, with paper records as the starting point.[^17]

We found no market-share claim for Care-wing.

## LIFE handling

The Care-wing home page says it "fully supports" LIFE ({{< ja "科学的介護情報システム" "kagakuteki kaigo jōhō shisutemu" "scientific care information system" >}}). It describes a colour-coded assessment screen and monitoring that can be imported from plans.[^8] The regular-visit page repeats the claim.[^9] The sources we opened do not say how Logic handled the May 2026 move of LIFE to Kokuho Chūōkai.

## Claims to kokuho-ren

Care-wing does not send claims to the federation ({{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}). MHLW's October 2025 survey entry for Care-wing leaves the claim-statement, invoice and transmission items unmarked.[^13] Logic's FAQ says claims are made through linked billing software.[^3]

## Care Plan Data Exchange

On 2 October 2026, Logic announced that Care-wing had completed Kokuho Chūōkai's vendor test. It reported no problems with sending, receiving or the import test.[^10] Kokuho Chūōkai's V4 vendor-test list shows Care-wing as having completed patterns **D2, D3, D6 and D7**.[^11] These are the planned and actual use tables for care management and for preventive care support; our [Care Plan Data Exchange guide](../../guides/care-plan-data-exchange/) explains the patterns. In MHLW's October 2025 survey, Logic marked standard version 3.2 as supported with the vendor test done, and version 4.1 as planned.[^13]

## Under M3

M3 bought Logic to use Care-wing as a "foothold" for digitising home-visit care and nursing, in M3's words, and planned to strengthen its sales with M3's network and web marketing.[^4] In its June 2026 deck on Wiseman, M3 said it would extend the AI results from Care-wing to Wiseman's products.[^17] M3's notice on the Wiseman deal says Logic already had a sales-consignment relationship with Wiseman.[^18] M3's 2024 annual securities report placed Logic in the Medical Platform segment.[^6]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: Care-wing is a records-first product for the home-visit workflow. It wins on one physical idea, touching a phone to a tag, and leaves billing to partners, including products owned by M3's rivals. Since M3 also bought Wiseman, the M3 group now has both a records specialist for home visits and a full package with its own home-visit products. How M3 positions the two against each other is not yet public. The time-saving figures are M3's own models, not measured results, and I would quote them only as such. Care-wing's V4 test covers the provider side of the exchange (D2, D3, D6, D7), which is what a home-visit records product needs.
{{< /analysis >}}
