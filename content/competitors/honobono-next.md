*Last updated: 9 Oct 2026*

Honobono NEXT ({{< ja "ほのぼのNEXT" "honobono nekusuto" "Honobono NEXT" >}}) is a care software package from ND Software Co., Ltd. ({{< ja "エヌ・デーソフトウェア株式会社" "enu dē sofutowea kabushiki-gaisha" "ND Software Co., Ltd." >}}), based in Nan'yō, Yamagata Prefecture.[^2] Its product page describes it as linking care records through to care-insurance claims.[^1] SOMPO Holdings made ND Software a wholly owned subsidiary on **28 February 2023**.[^2]

## At a glance

| Item | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| Vendor | ND Software Co., Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of SOMPO Holdings since 28 Feb 2023[^2] |
| Pricing model | Five-year software licence. Fee-revision updates included at no extra charge.[^1] |
| Published price | None. The cost depends on the office's size and needs, and is quoted on request.[^1] |
| Customer count | More than 72,300 offices across the whole series, ND Software alone, April 2026 (ND Software's own count)[^1] |
| Share claims | SOMPO estimated in March 2023 that ND Software held about 35% of special nursing homes and paid homes (special nursing homes alone: 43%), about 35% of geriatric health facilities and group homes, and about 20% of home-based offices. This is SOMPO's estimate, not an independent market share.[^3] |
| LIFE | A "LIFE" menu that compiles daily records into CSV output[^4] |
| Claims to kokuho-ren | Records linked to care-insurance claims[^1]. The transmission method is not described in the sources we opened. |
| Care Plan Data Exchange | Completed V4 vendor-test patterns D1–D7[^5] |

## Pricing

ND Software does not publish a price list. Its product page says installation costs vary with the office's size and requirements, and asks prospective customers to make an enquiry.[^1] The page says the Honobono series is sold as a **five-year software licence** ({{< ja "ソフトウェア使用権" "sofutowea shiyō-ken" "software usage right" >}}). On that basis, it says, fee revisions do not cost extra and future software costs are easier to predict.[^1] A 2024 ND Software notice on LIFE says that updated versions were provided to customers whose time-limited licence pack was still within its term.[^4]

## Main features

The product page and an ND Software release list these features:[^1][^6]

- **Coverage.** A lineup covering care insurance, disability welfare, accounting and payroll.[^1]
- **Records.** Tablet record entry, voice input ("Care Palette", "Voice fun") and a bone-conduction intercom.[^1]
- **AI and sensors.** An AI care plan tool, and an IoT cloud that links bed and monitoring sensors to records.[^1]
- **Cloud and family app.** A cloud version ("online platform"), and a family app ("Tsunagaru Kazoku", {{< ja "つながる家族" "tsunagaru kazoku" "connected family" >}}) for sharing bills and notices.[^1]
- **Honobono ONE, coming in late 2026.** On 19 June 2026, ND Software announced three cloud products called "Honobono ONE" ({{< ja "ほのぼのONE" "honobono wan" "Honobono ONE" >}}). They run alongside Honobono NEXT on its online platform:[^6]
  - a care-manager tool for browser and tablet
  - Care Palette PRO for recording on iOS
  - a facility operations management tool

  Sales are planned to start around autumn 2026, with formal shipping in autumn to winter 2026. Prices have not been announced.[^6]

## Target customers

The product page offers different products by office type, plus a separate proposal for corporations with many offices.[^1] SOMPO's March 2023 strategy deck breaks ND Software's customer base down by segment:[^3]

| Segment (SOMPO's grouping) | Market size (SOMPO's estimate) | ND customers | Share (SOMPO's estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid homes and special nursing homes | about 2,500 facilities (mid-to-large operators) plus about 12,000 facilities (small operators) | about 5,500 facilities | about 35% (special nursing homes alone: 43%) |
| Geriatric health facilities and group homes | about 18,000 facilities | about 7,000 facilities | about 35% |
| Home-based (care management, small-scale multifunctional, nursing small-scale multifunctional) | about 45,000 offices | about 9,000 offices | about 20% |

The same deck gives the background figures below. All of them are SOMPO's, for the year to March 2022 or earlier:[^3]

- ND Software's consolidated revenue was **¥19.21 billion**.
- Its software ran at about **50,000** care offices.
- Sales were split as follows: about 55% through ten large dealers, about 20% through about 700 small dealers, and about 25% direct.

## LIFE handling

In June 2024, ND Software said Honobono NEXT's "LIFE" menu compiles data from daily records and other entries and outputs it as a CSV file. Data already entered was converted for the post-2024 LIFE system.[^4] The sources we opened do not cover how ND Software handled the May 2026 move of LIFE to Kokuho Chūōkai.

## Claims to kokuho-ren

The product page describes Honobono NEXT as linking care records to care-insurance claims. The page's menu also lists a separate claims-administration support service.[^1] The sources we opened do not say how claims are transmitted to the federation ({{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}).

## Care Plan Data Exchange

Kokuho Chūōkai's V4 vendor-test list shows Honobono NEXT as having completed all seven patterns, D1–D7. It also lists ND Software's community support centre system, which completed D4–D7.[^5] "Honobono mini2" from Nippon Care Communications completed D1–D3.[^5] SOMPO's 2023 release names Nippon Care Communications as an affiliated company of ND Software.[^2]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: on SOMPO's own estimates, Honobono is strongest in facilities, especially special nursing homes. That is the opposite of Kaipoke's home-care focus. The five-year licence and the dealer-led sales model are signs of an on-premise heritage. "Honobono ONE" looks like ND Software's answer to cloud-first rivals, and it is built to sit alongside NEXT rather than replace it. The 2023 share estimates are now more than three years old and come from the acquirer, so they should be treated as positioning, not current market data.
