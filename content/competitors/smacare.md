*Last updated: 9 Oct 2026*

SmaCare ({{< ja "スマケア" "sumakea" "SmaCare" >}}) is a business-support system for one service type: 24-hour regular and on-call home-visit care and nursing ({{< ja "定期巡回・随時対応型訪問介護看護" "teiki junkai zuiji taiō-gata hōmon kaigo kango" "regular-visit and on-call home care and nursing" >}}).[^1] It is a **records** system. Claims are made in separate billing software that links to it.[^2] It is provided by Homenet Co., Ltd. ({{< ja "ホームネット株式会社" "hōmunetto kabushiki-gaisha" "Homenet Co., Ltd." >}}) in Nakano, Tokyo. Homenet's group also runs regular-visit services of its own.[^3]

## Pricing

We did not find a price list on the SmaCare site. Homenet's FAQ says offices can use prefectural and municipal ICT subsidies to keep the initial cost down. It adds that call windows and conditions vary by local government.[^7] On 1 September 2026, Homenet announced that SmaCare had been registered in the Welfare Equipment Information System (TAIS) ({{< ja "福祉用具情報システム" "fukushi yōgu jōhō shisutemu" "Welfare Equipment Information System" >}}). Homenet says this makes SmaCare easier to use as a system eligible for ICT subsidies.[^8] Our [subsidy guide]({{< relref "guides/subsidies-and-software-buying" >}}) explains how TAIS listing relates to the subsidy.

## Main features

- **Schedules and records on a smartphone.** Staff check schedules and record actuals in an app. Records are shared at once with others involved, and user information is collected in one place.[^1]
- **Sharing outside the office.** Homenet can issue SmaCare accounts to users' families, care managers and other care offices, so they see records in real time.[^4]
- **Emergency calls.** When a user presses a call button, the user's details pop up on the operator's PC. Call times are logged and calls are recorded, and operators can be centralised.[^4]
- **Inspection support.** Items checked in municipal operational guidance ({{< ja "運営指導" "un'ei shidō" "operational guidance (inspection)" >}}) are gathered in one place.[^4]
- **Multilingual app.** Since September 2025 the records app can display in 17 languages, including Japanese, with care terms translated in advance. Homenet presents this as helping offices that employ foreign staff.[^3]
- **"SmaCare AI" option.** It has two parts:[^9]
  - a management function that analyses actuals data automatically
  - a field function that drafts special notes, proofreads records and drafts care and nursing plans

  For a model office with 30 users, Homenet claims savings of about **60 hours a month**, and says plan drafting falls from 60 minutes to 12 per plan. These are vendor figures.[^9]
- **"SmaCare Lite: Home-Visit Care".** A simpler, AI-equipped edition for ordinary home-visit care offices, built on the regular-visit product.[^10]

## Links to other software

Homenet's link page lists four external systems:[^5]

- **TriCare-Tops.** Homenet describes it as offering a data-transmission agency service to the federation. TriCare-Tops' own page says it sends staff and user information to SmaCare and imports actuals from it.[^5][^11]
- **CAREKARTE.** Visits registered in CAREKARTE can be registered in SmaCare. CAREKARTE lists the link from its side too.[^5][^12]
- **QuaLink.** A video-call system whose call history is written into SmaCare.[^5]
- **Quick Care** ({{< ja "Quickけあ" "kuikku kea" "Quick Care" >}}).[^5]

## Target customers

The main product targets offices providing regular-visit and on-call services.[^1] SmaCare Lite targets home-visit care offices, including home-care departments inside larger corporations.[^10] Homenet says SmaCare has been offered since the regular-visit service type was created.[^3]

## Scale and share: what Homenet claims

All figures here are Homenet's own:

- **Current claim.** The SmaCare home page says SmaCare was in use at **700 offices** at the end of May 2026. It puts this at **about 47.2%** of **1,483** regular-visit offices that billed. Homenet estimates that base from MHLW's survey of long-term care benefit expenditure for the May 2026 review month.[^1] We did not check the 1,483 figure against the MHLW statistics.
- **Earlier claim.** In September 2025, Homenet said more than 650 offices used SmaCare.[^3]
- **"Top-class" share.** The site also calls its share "top-class" in this field. That is Homenet's own wording, based on its own research and estimates.[^1]

## LIFE handling

We found no LIFE function in the SmaCare pages we opened. SmaCare is not listed in MHLW's August 2026 care-software function survey.[^13]

## Claims to kokuho-ren

SmaCare does not make claims to the federation ({{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}) itself. Homenet's Care Plan Data Exchange notice says SmaCare is a records system. Its actuals therefore carry no billing information such as add-on and reduction codes, and offices must output billing information from their billing system.[^2] The TriCare-Tops link brings SmaCare actuals into that billing software.[^11]

## Care Plan Data Exchange

On 29 June 2026, Homenet announced that SmaCare had passed Kokuho Chūōkai's vendor test for the exchange. The test confirmed that sending, receiving and importing data worked, and Homenet planned to release the function in July 2026.[^2] Kokuho Chūōkai's V4 vendor-test list shows Homenet's SmaCare as having completed patterns D1–D7.[^6] Because SmaCare's actuals lack billing codes, Homenet tells users to send the billing information needed by care-management offices from their billing system.[^2]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: SmaCare shows what a single-service-type specialist looks like in Japan. It goes deep on one workflow (round-the-clock visits plus emergency calls) and leaves billing to partners. Its 47.2% claim is Homenet's own estimate. Even so, it suggests a small segment (around 1,500 offices by Homenet's count) can be covered by one focused vendor. Two moves are worth watching: the multilingual app, aimed at offices hiring foreign staff, and the push into ordinary home-visit care with SmaCare Lite. The split between records and billing is also a live issue for the Care Plan Data Exchange. Homenet's own notice says actuals from a records-only system cannot carry billing codes.
{{< /analysis >}}
