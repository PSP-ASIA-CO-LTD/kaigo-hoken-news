*Last updated: 9 Oct 2026*

Wiseman ({{< ja "ワイズマン" "waizuman" "Wiseman" >}}) is a software company based in Morioka, Iwate Prefecture. Founded in June 1983, it develops, sells and supports systems for care and welfare offices, medical institutions and local governments.[^1] On **5 June 2026**, M3, Inc. ({{< ja "エムスリー株式会社" "emu surī kabushiki-gaisha" "M3, Inc." >}}, TSE Prime 2413) signed an agreement to buy all of Wiseman's shares. The transfer was scheduled for **1 July 2026**.[^1][^2] M3's Report for Investors 2026, dated 30 September 2026, says Wiseman was consolidated as a subsidiary "from July" 2026. None of the documents we opened gives the exact day.[^7] Our page on [the M3 group in care]({{< relref "m3-group" >}}) covers the deal terms and M3's other care companies.

## Pricing

Wiseman's pricing page gives no list prices. Prospective customers ask for a free quote, and Wiseman prepares one for each office.[^3] The page describes two charges:[^3]

- **Initial installation fee.** Setup fees and fees for on-site operating instructions, priced to the customer's needs.
- **Five-year usage-right pack** ({{< ja "5年間使用権パック" "go-nenkan shiyō-ken pakku" "five-year usage-right pack" >}}). Paid at contract and at each renewal. It includes the system licence, maintenance, law-change updates (the page cites a revision once every three years), version upgrades and renewals. Wiseman says these updates bring no additional charge.

## Main features

- **Coverage.** The care and welfare product line covers care management, facility services (geriatric health facilities, special nursing homes, specified facilities), small-scale multifunctional services and nursing small-scale multifunctional services, among others. It includes record-support software, and Wiseman also sells to disability-welfare services.[^4]
- **Claims and records.** The core package handles claims processing, summary reports and tablet-based care records, and covers care benefits, preventive benefits and the comprehensive community programme.[^4]
- **ASP option.** The product page lists an ASP (hosted) service.[^4]
- **Medical-care link (MeLL+).** "MeLL+" ({{< ja "メルタス" "merutasu" "MeLL+" >}}) connects medical institutions, care facilities and families.[^4]
- **Voice-record AI.** On 24 March 2026, Wiseman released an option that turns what staff say into an intercom app into care-record entries. It uses the "BONX WORK" app.[^6]

## Target customers

M3's acquisition deck describes Wiseman as having two businesses:[^2]

- a **welfare business** that streamlines record management and claims for care facilities
- a **medical business** that sells electronic health records and medical-office systems to small and mid-sized hospitals and clinics

The deck gives these figures for the company:[^2]

- **Revenue** of **¥12,377 million** and an **operating loss** of **¥222 million** for the year to June 2025.
- A **forecast** of ¥13,980 million revenue and ¥1,586 million operating profit for the year to June 2026.
- **535 employees** at the end of June 2025, and 18 branches.

M3's TDnet notice gives Wiseman's sales for the three years to June 2023, 2024 and 2025 as ¥11,354 million, ¥12,202 million and ¥12,377 million. Operating results were a profit of ¥457 million, then losses of ¥346 million and ¥222 million.[^8]

These figures cover both businesses, not care software alone.

## Share: what M3 claims

M3's deck has a slide titled "Wiseman's share of the care market". It gives the figures below without any stated method, source or date:[^2]

| Segment | M3's stated share |
|---|---|
| Geriatric health facilities ({{< ja "介護老人保健施設" "kaigo rōjin hoken shisetsu" "geriatric health services facility (rōken)" >}}) | about 40% |
| Special nursing homes ({{< ja "介護老人福祉施設" "kaigo rōjin fukushi shisetsu" "special nursing home (tokuyō)" >}}) | about 25% |
| Group homes for dementia ({{< ja "認知症対応型共同生活介護" "ninchishō taiō-gata kyōdō seikatsu kaigo" "dementia group home" >}}) | about 15% |
| Care-management offices ({{< ja "居宅介護支援" "kyotaku kaigo shien" "home-care support (care management)" >}}) | about 15% |
| Home-visit care offices | about 10% |
| Home-visit nursing offices | about 15% |

These are the buyer's figures, published to explain an acquisition. They should not be read as measured market shares.

## LIFE handling

Wiseman's product page says the package supports input and output of the forms for LIFE ({{< ja "科学的介護情報システム" "kagakuteki kaigo jōhō shisutemu" "scientific care information system" >}}).[^4] The sources we opened do not say how Wiseman handled the May 2026 handover of LIFE to Kokuho Chūōkai.

## Claims to kokuho-ren

The product page describes reliable claims processing and claims for all benefit types.[^4] The sources we opened do not describe how claims are transmitted to the federation ({{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}).

## Care Plan Data Exchange

Kokuho Chūōkai's V4 vendor-test list shows "Wiseman SP System" as having completed all seven patterns, D1–D7.[^5]

## What changes under M3

M3's deck sets out plans to:[^2]

- use M3's AI to add value to Wiseman products, extending AI results from "Care-wing", a product of Logic ({{< ja "ロジック" "rojikku" "Logic" >}}), which M3 lists among its group's care services (see our [Care-wing profile]({{< relref "care-wing" >}}))
- link Wiseman's MeLL+ with M3's cloud electronic health record, "M3 Digikar" ({{< ja "エムスリーデジカル" "emu surī dejikaru" "M3 Digikar" >}})
- use M3's marketing to help sell Wiseman products

Wiseman told customers that current services, support and contracts would not change significantly after it joined the M3 group.[^1]

M3's notice says two M3 subsidiaries, Logic and CUC, already had transactions with Wiseman relating to consignment of product sales.[^8] In its August 2026 results presentation, M3 described Wiseman, in M3's words, as "positioned as the platform for realising the 'one person, one yen' mission in the care field as well".[^9]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: going by their owners' own share claims, Wiseman and Honobono are both facility-heavy, licence-based packages. Wiseman now has a parent with an AI and medical-records business, and with other care services in its group (Logic and Elan, per M3). That combination could make the M3 group a stronger all-round competitor than Wiseman alone, especially for links between medical and care providers. The share figures come from the buyer's acquisition deck. I would cite them only as M3's claim. M3's own investor report says the deal took effect in July 2026, but I have not seen the exact day confirmed.
