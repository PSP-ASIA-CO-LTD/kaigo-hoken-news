*Last updated: 9 Oct 2026*

Rehab Cloud ({{< ja "リハブクラウド" "rihabu kuraudo" "Rehab Cloud" >}}) is run by Rehab for JAPAN Co., Ltd., based in Chiyoda, Tokyo. The company calls it "scientific care software" ({{< ja "科学的介護ソフト" "kagakuteki kaigo sofuto" "scientific care software" >}}).[^1] Its main market is day services. In 2026 it extended the product to special nursing homes and specified facilities.[^1]

## Pricing

The pricing page lists three things that cost **¥0**: the initial fee, maintenance and contract renewal. The base fee "varies by plan". Rehab for JAPAN asks prospective customers to download a price list for the actual figures.[^2] The page also says:[^2]

- Customers can choose a monthly or an annual contract, and no cancellation fee applies.
- No additional charges arise unless the customer changes plan or adds options.
- System updates for changes in fee requirements are free.

We did not obtain the downloadable price list, so no figure is quoted here.

## Main features

The product page lists the following:[^3]

- **Planning.** Automatically suggests goals and exercise programmes for each user. A functional-training plan can be produced in as little as three minutes.
- **AI tools.** AI motion analysis and AI text generation.
- **Add-ons supported.** Individual functional training, scientific care system, ADL maintenance, oral function improvement, bathing assistance and nutrition assessment, with the related forms and LIFE output.
- **Integrated billing.** Care records, planning and claims are handled in one system.

Rehab for JAPAN has also announced these 2026 additions:

- **Nutrition assessment (26 May 2026).** A nutrition-assessment add-on ({{< ja "栄養アセスメント加算" "eiyō asesumento kasan" "nutrition assessment add-on" >}}) function that fills in about 80% of the 70 assessment items automatically and supports submission to LIFE.[^6]
- **Online speech-therapy assessment (from September 2026).** "Rehab Cloud Online ST", in which speech-language-hearing therapists assess users' oral function online.[^7]

## Target customers

Rehab for JAPAN says it built the product for day services ({{< ja "通所介護" "tsūsho kaigo" "day care service" >}}). Its aim was that offices without rehabilitation professionals could still meet add-on requirements.[^1] On 13 July 2026, it announced that it would offer Rehab Cloud to two new types of facility from summer 2026:[^1]

- special nursing homes ({{< ja "特別養護老人ホーム" "tokubetsu yōgo rōjin hōmu" "special nursing home" >}})
- specified facilities ({{< ja "特定施設" "tokutei shisetsu" "specified facility" >}})

## Scale: what Rehab for JAPAN reports

The company reports a cumulative total of more than **4,316** offices at the end of June 2026.[^1] This is a cumulative adoption count that the company published, not a market share.

## LIFE handling

The product page says basic information and ADL assessments flow into the LIFE report forms. The office then completes submission by importing the CSV file into LIFE ({{< ja "科学的介護情報システム" "kagakuteki kaigo jōhō shisutemu" "scientific care information system" >}}).[^3] The nutrition-assessment function released in May 2026 also covers LIFE submission.[^6] The sources we opened do not describe how Rehab Cloud handled the May 2026 handover of LIFE to Kokuho Chūōkai.

## Claims to kokuho-ren

The product page says records and claims are linked, so that anyone can handle claims to the federation ({{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}).[^3]

## Care Plan Data Exchange

Rehab Cloud says it supports the Care Plan Data Exchange (V4). The documents it exchanges are care plan tables 1 to 3, the provision table and its annex.[^4] For care managers who do not use the exchange, users can fax documents directly from the screen. This includes the monitoring report, which the exchange does not carry.[^4] Kokuho Chūōkai's V4 vendor-test list shows Rehab Cloud as having completed all seven patterns, D1–D7.[^5]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: Rehab Cloud is the clearest example among these five of a product built around add-ons and LIFE rather than around the claim. It sells revenue from add-ons and outcomes, then adds billing so that the office does not need a second system. The 2026 moves into special nursing homes and online speech therapy show it pushing into higher-value add-ons. It is far smaller than the incumbents by its own count, and that is the scale a focused newcomer could realistically aim for.
