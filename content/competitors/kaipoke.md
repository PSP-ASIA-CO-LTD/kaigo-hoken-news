Kaipoke ({{< ja "カイポケ" "kaipoke" "Kaipoke" >}}) is cloud-based management-support software for care and disability-welfare offices. It is run by SMS Co., Ltd. ({{< ja "株式会社エス・エム・エス" "kabushiki-gaisha esu emu esu" "SMS Co., Ltd." >}}), which is listed on the Prime market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange (code 2175).[^1]

## At a glance

| Item | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| Vendor | SMS Co., Ltd., TSE Prime 2175[^1] |
| Pricing model | Flat monthly fee per office location. No fee for initial setup, support, law-change updates, claim transmission, renewal or cancellation.[^2] |
| Published price range | ¥1,000–¥25,000 a month per service, excluding tax[^2] |
| Free trial | 2 months[^3] |
| Focus | Home-based services. SMS says Kaipoke's management-support product does not cover facility-type services such as group homes.[^1] |
| Customer count | 62,300 offices and 27,600 corporations at the end of June 2026 (Kaipoke and Kabenashi Cloud ({{< ja "かべなしクラウド" "kabenashi kuraudo" "Kabenashi Cloud" >}}) combined, SMS figures)[^1] |
| LIFE | Outputs CSV that can be imported into LIFE (day-care functional-training documents)[^4] |
| Claims to kokuho-ren | Electronic claim transmission included at no extra charge[^2] |
| Care Plan Data Exchange | Supports standard 4.1, both import and export[^5]. Completed V4 vendor-test patterns D1–D7.[^6] |

## Pricing

Several services at the same address can be bundled at a special price, quoted on request.[^2] An SMS-run Kaipoke column lists Kaipoke as "from ¥5,000 a month (excluding tax)". That figure is the care-management rate, not the rate for home-visit care or day care.[^7]

## Main features

- **One system from records to claims.** Records, form creation and claims are linked, with tablet support for most service types.[^2][^3]
- **AI and RPA through a partner.** On 25 March 2026, SMS announced a strategic partnership with Welmo. Kaipoke users can add three Welmo services on a separate contract:[^8]
  - AI transcription and summaries of visit conversations in statutory record formats
  - AI for creating forms and other paperwork
  - RPA that automatically transfers AI-generated data into Kaipoke
- **"Kaipoke Connect".** In its April 2026 value-creation roadmap, SMS describes a plan to turn Kaipoke into a network hub linking offices, other vendors' software and medical institutions.[^9]

## Target customers

SMS says Kaipoke's management-support product covers home-based offices: care management, home-visit care, day care, home-visit nursing, day rehabilitation and similar. It does **not** cover facility-type services such as group homes, short stays, specified facilities and small-scale multifunctional services.[^1] The price list also covers several disability-welfare services.[^2]

## Scale and share: what SMS claims

All figures in this section are SMS's own. None is an independent market share.

- **Customers.** At the end of June 2026, Kaipoke and Kabenashi Cloud together had **27,600** customer corporations and **62,300** customer offices. ARPA (average revenue per account) was **¥121,000** and ARR (annual recurring revenue) was **¥13.5 billion**.[^1]
- **Segment results.** SMS's care and disability-welfare management-support segment had first-quarter revenue of **¥3,474 million**, up 9% on the year before.[^1]
- **SMS-defined shares.** SMS reports a "paid membership share" of **14%** among elderly-care and disability-care offices. It also reports a "home-care connection share" of **62%**, which it defines as paid plus free members among home-care offices. Its target for the year to March 2031 is **90% or more**. Both shares are SMS's own calculations.[^9]
- **Home-visit nursing.** In March 2026, SMS said "Kaipoke Home-Visit Nursing" had passed **7,000** offices, which it described as more than a third of home-visit nursing stations nationwide.[^10]

## LIFE handling

For day care, Kaipoke says it outputs CSV files that can be imported into LIFE ({{< ja "科学的介護情報システム" "kagakuteki kaigo jōhō shisutemu" "scientific care information system" >}}). These include the individual functional training plan and the life-function check sheet. Kaipoke presents this as support for the functional-training and oral-function add-ons.[^4] The sources we opened do not show which other LIFE forms Kaipoke exports for other service types.

## Claims to kokuho-ren

The price list includes internet claim transmission ({{< ja "伝送" "densō" "electronic claim transmission" >}}) to the federation ({{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}) at **¥0**.[^2]

## Care Plan Data Exchange

Kaipoke says it officially supports version 4.1 of the care-plan data standard, for both output and import of CSV data.[^5] Kokuho Chūōkai's V4 vendor-test list shows Kaipoke as having completed all seven patterns, D1–D7.[^6]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: Kaipoke competes on published, low, flat prices for home-based services, and on a free tier that SMS counts as "connection share". The ¥5,000 figure that circulates is only the care-management rate. A home-visit or day-care office pays ¥25,000. SMS states that Kaipoke does not cover facility-type services. If that is accurate, facility-type services are the clearest gap in Kaipoke's coverage. "Kaipoke Connect" shows SMS wants to own the links between offices, not just the record.
