Kanamic ({{< ja "カナミック" "kanamikku" "Kanamic" >}}) is a cloud service for medical and care offices, run by Kanamic Network Co., Ltd. ({{< ja "株式会社カナミックネットワーク" "kabushiki-gaisha kanamikku nettowāku" "Kanamic Network Co., Ltd." >}}). The company was founded on 20 October 2000 and is listed on the Prime market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange (code 3939).[^1] Its pages describe the service as covering daily care records through to management reporting, with information sharing across organisations.[^2]

## Pricing

Kanamic's marketing page says customers pay an "initial installation fee" and a "monthly usage fee". These cover system setup, phone support, server maintenance and law-change updates. Kanamic applies fee-revision updates itself at no charge to users. The page also says use is billed monthly per office, with an unlimited number of accounts for some services.[^1] We did not find a published price list on the Kanamic pages we opened.[^1][^2]

## Main features

- **Coverage.** The care-software page groups functions into three areas:[^2]
  - management: visualising sales and receipts, extracting analysis data, attendance and payroll
  - team collaboration: care chat, meeting scheduling, online meetings
  - care records: touch and voice input, QR scanning, image and video sharing

  It covers most home services, facilities (including special nursing homes, geriatric health facilities and group homes), serviced housing and disability services.[^2]
- **Options.** Optional modules include receivables management, account transfer, BI analysis, electronic invoices and mailing, electronic payment and an AI visit-route tool.[^2] Kanamic launched the AI visit-route tool for home-visit care and nursing offices on 11 July 2024.[^7]
- **AI releases in 2026.** Kanamic's May 2026 results deck lists three new AI functions:[^3]
  - support for writing home-visit-care monitoring reports (announced 7 April 2026)
  - AI meeting minutes
  - AI-OCR that reads care-insurance cards and fills in the data automatically

## Target customers

Kanamic's service list covers community comprehensive support centres, care-management offices, home services, community-based services, facilities and disability services.[^2] For its medical and care cloud business, its results deck lists four customer types: municipalities, medical associations, medical corporations and care providers.[^3]

## Scale: what Kanamic reports

All figures in this section are Kanamic's own.

- **Half-year results.** For the six months to March 2026, consolidated revenue was **¥3,134 million**, up 17.8% on the year before. Operating profit was **¥1,000 million**.[^3]
- **Customers.** Kanamic reports **57,763** offices and **376,972** user IDs. It attributes the large rise in users and offices to wider use of the Care Plan Data Exchange.[^3]
- **Regions.** The cloud service is used in **1,420** of about 4,095 "regions". Kanamic defines a region as a junior-high-school district of about 30,000 people, the unit MHLW assumes for community-based integrated care.[^3]
- **Overseas.** Kanamic completed a fiscal 2025 project for the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications. The project tested its health, medical and care information-sharing system in Singapore.[^3]

## LIFE handling

Kanamic's care-software page lists support for the LIFE scientific-care system ({{< ja "科学的介護情報システム" "kagakuteki kaigo jōhō shisutemu" "scientific care information system" >}}) among its benefits.[^2] The sources we opened do not describe which LIFE forms Kanamic exports or how it handled the May 2026 handover to Kokuho Chūōkai.

## Claims to kokuho-ren

Kanamic describes the following claims workflow for facilities and serviced housing:[^4]

1. The facility confirms and locks its service records.
2. Head office downloads the claim data for the federation ({{< ja "国保連" "kokuho-ren" "prefectural National Health Insurance federation" >}}) in bulk and transmits it.
3. Receivables are reconciled automatically from the federation's payment results and bank-transfer results.

## Care Plan Data Exchange

Since the fiscal 2024 revision, care-management fee type II requires use of the Care Plan Data Exchange or of a system that MHLW's review panel accepts as equivalent.[^5] On 8 April 2025, Kanamic announced that its cloud service had been accepted, describing itself as "the only private company" to be adopted.[^8] MHLW's page now lists **four** approved systems: Kanamic's and three others.[^5] The "only" claim is therefore out of date. Kokuho Chūōkai's V4 vendor-test list also shows the Kanamic cloud service as having completed all seven patterns, D1–D7.[^6]

{{< analysis title="Tako-San's take" >}}
Tako-San's view: Kanamic sells the network as much as the record. Its pitch is information sharing across a whole district, and it counts "regions" as well as offices. The equivalence approval let care managers on Kanamic qualify for fee type II without using the national exchange directly. That advantage looks smaller now that three other systems are approved and the national exchange is becoming free inside the Kaigo WEB Service from 2027. Of the sources I read for these five vendors, the Singapore project is the clearest sign of export ambitions in Asia.
