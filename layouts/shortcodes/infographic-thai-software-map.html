CloudNurse

Vendor: TH Healthtech Co., Ltd., per press coverage; the website footer says "CloudNurse Co., Ltd.". Launched November 2024.

Target: private nursing homes.

Pricing: per resident per month: Basic 300 baht per resident per month, up to 50 residents; Enterprise on request.

Links with the state: NHSO LTC programme, 3C and DHSS not published. "API integration" in Enterprise only.

LINE and mobile: family reports and notifications through LINE; browser and mobile.

Scale claim: "35+" homes, "300+" resident accounts (vendor, undated).

Source: CloudNurse profile · Feature matrix

Caleo

Vendor: not named as a company on Caleo's site. Launched in 2026; "Founding Provider phase".

Target: care homes, nursing homes, home care, day care, long-term care, rehabilitation.

Pricing: per bed; no price published, quoted after a demo.

Links with the state: claims its records map to Thai standards and produce "evidence packs" for DHSS inspection, the only vendor whose pages mention DHSS. NHSO LTC programme and 3C not published.

LINE and mobile: LINE Family Portal; mobile carer app that works offline.

Source: Caleo profile · Feature matrix

HexaHealth (elderly-care module)

Vendor: "HexaHealth Co., Ltd." per its privacy policy; a clinic and hospital platform with an elderly-care module.

Target: nursing homes, day care, home care, memory care, hospice, senior living.

Pricing: bed-band tiers: Free (up to 5 beds), 1,990, 3,490 and 5,900 baht. The page does not say whether these are monthly. The same page also says "from 5,000 THB/month for 20 residents". The two contradict each other; ask for a written quote.

Links with the state: the clinic claims module has an NHSO payer setting; this is not the LTC fund. 3C and DHSS not published. REST API and webhooks.

LINE: daily LINE report to family (Care Starter tier).

Source: HexaHealth profile · Feature matrix

APSX (APS Thailand)

Target: clinics, hospitals, spas, wellness and elderly-care centres; an elderly-care-centre edition of a clinic platform.

Pricing: annual package by branches and users; residents unlimited. 27,000, 54,000 and 135,000 baht a year, excluding VAT. Add-ons are priced "depending on usage".

Links with the state: NHSO LTC programme, 3C and DHSS not published. REST API advertised.

LINE and mobile: families follow through the centre's LINE OA, no app; tablet logging; web browser.

Scale claim: "1,000+ branches" across all business types (vendor, undated).

Source: APSX profile · Feature matrix

MCS (Miracle Clinic System, Everyday Doctor Co., Ltd.)

Target: clinics, wellness centres, elderly-care centres, private hospitals.

Pricing: annual package by users and features: six clinic packages from 9,900 to 35,900 baht a year. The elderly-care price is not published.

Links with the state: NHSO LTC programme, 3C and DHSS not published. Accounting-system links; "API to external systems" from the Gold package.

LINE and mobile: LINE OA from the Silver package, 3,000 baht a year on the Standard tiers; cloud, "every device".

Scale claim: "2,000+" clinics (vendor, undated).

Source: MCS profile · Feature matrix

NEC Mimamori (past trial)

In June 2022 NEC Corporation (Thailand) ran a month-long proof of concept at an elderly-care facility in Chiang Mai: an online care-plan manager, a family app, and fall-detection sensors using 4D imaging radar.

NEC said workloads fell by 36% for the care manager and 48% for direct caregivers. These are the vendor's own figures from one site over one month.

In 2023 Chiang Mai municipality and NEC Thailand signed a smart-city memorandum. No later public report on the trial or the memorandum was found. Pricing not published.

NEC is not in our feature matrix, so it has no badges.

Source: Government systems: NEC Mimamori

Nirun and Nirun for Community (NSTDA, with DOP)

Target: DOP's 12 welfare centres (Nirun) and DOP's community caregivers (Nirun for Community), 268 areas in 76 provinces in 2026.

Pricing: licensed through NSTDA's Technology Licensing Office ("seeking licensees"); no price published.

Links with the state: NHSO LTC programme, 3C and DHSS not published.

LINE and mobile: Nirun for Community is a field app that runs through LINE, per a press report. LINE for the centres' system is not published.

Source: Nirun profile · Feature matrix

Department of Health 3C program

Target: care managers in the NHSO community scheme, who register caregivers and write care plans.

Pricing: not published (state system for scheme staff).

Links with the state: pulls the list of dependent people from the NHSO LTC programme; 2018 data agreement with the NHSO. DHSS: not applicable. "3C PLUS", linked with the NHSO, reported for 2027 (news).

Mobile and LINE: web; LINE not published.

Source: Government systems · Feature matrix

NHSO LTC programme (ltcnew.nhso.go.th)

Target: service units and local governments in the NHSO community scheme.

Pricing: not published (state system for scheme staff).

Links with the state: no link to 3C found; approved plans are re-entered by the local government. DHSS: not applicable.

Records: each approved plan's ADL score, care group and TAI; agreements, vouchers, payment records; monthly caregiver pay under the 2025 stimulus project.

Mobile and LINE: web; LINE not published.

Source: Government systems · Feature matrix

Thai COC (continuity of care)

Target: hospitals and primary-care units; a hospital-to-community referral tool, not care-home software.

Pricing: no charge to users (2018 report).

Links with the state: NHSO LTC programme and 3C not published. DHSS: not applicable.

Mobile and LINE: home-visit records in a mobile app; automatic LINE alerts to staff.

Scale: 39 provinces, 4,898 units, 292,155 patients by 10 October 2018; no newer figure found.

Source: Government systems · Feature matrix

Blue Book app (สมุดบันทึกสุขภาพผู้สูงอายุ, samut banthuek sukkhaphap phu sung-ayu, elderly health record book)

Target: older people, health staff, volunteers and local-government staff; Department of Health.

Pricing: not published; no price shown on the store listing.

Links with the state: NHSO LTC programme and 3C not published. DHSS: not applicable. Stated aim: share screening data with partner agencies.

Mobile and LINE: iOS and Android apps, web; LINE OA "@bluebook".

Source: Government systems · Feature matrix

Smart อสม. (DHSS)

Target: village health volunteers, อาสาสมัครสาธารณสุขประจำหมู่บ้าน (asasamak satharanasuk pracham muban, อสม.).

Pricing: not published; no price shown on the store listing.

Links with the state: NHSO LTC programme and 3C not published. DHSS: not applicable (DHSS publishes the app, but it is not for DHSS-licensed homes).

Mobile and LINE: Android app (iOS not checked); LINE not published. Monthly อสม. 1 work report.

Source: Government systems · Feature matrix
