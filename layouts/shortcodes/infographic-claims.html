Step 1: Care Plan Created
Before the service month, the care manager writes the care plan (ケアプラン, kea puran) with goals, services, and fees. The user gives written consent. The plan is delivered to the user and each provider.
Source: Monthly Cycle Guide
Step 2: Provision Table Sent
The care manager sends the service provision table (サービス提供票, sābisu teikyō-hyō) to each provider, showing the planned visits for the month. This can go via paper/fax or via the Care Plan Data Exchange (ケアプランデータ連携, kea puran dēta renkei). The annual ¥21,000 fee is waived for all of FY2026 under the free-pass campaign; the exchange is planned to merge into the Kaigo WEB Service around January 2027 (exact date not announced).
Source: Care Plan Data Exchange Guide
Step 3: Services Delivered & Recorded
During the month, providers deliver services and record each one: date, time, service type, staff, and content. Care software (介護記録ソフト, kaigo kiroku sofuto) typically links these records to claim data (for example, CAREKARTE creates claim data automatically from records). For the scientific-care system add-on (科学的介護推進体制加算, kagakuteki kaigo suishin taisei kasan), facilities and day-care services submit data to LIFE (科学的介護情報システム, kagakuteki kaigo jōhō shisutemu) at least every 3 months; the add-on is 40–60 units/month depending on service type.
Source: LIFE Guide
Step 4: Actuals Table Returned
Every month, care-management offices and providers exchange the planned and the actual use (サービス利用票・提供票, sābisu riyō-hyō, teikyō-hyō). By the 10th of the following month, the care manager files the benefit-management form (給付管理票, kyūfu kanri-hyō).
Source: Monthly Cycle Guide
Step 5: Claims Filed by the 10th
By the 10th of M+1 (hard deadline), two filings are due:
  • The provider's claim (介護給付費請求, kaigo kyūfu-hi seikyū) to the kokuho-ren
  • The care manager's benefit-management form (給付管理票, kyūfu kanri-hyō)
Miyagi kokuho-ren: electronic claims accepted until 17:15 on the 10th, including weekends and holidays. Late claims are treated as next month's.
Source: Monthly Cycle Guide
Step 6: Kokuho-ren Reviews
The prefectural kokuho-ren checks the claim against the care manager's benefit-management form. If errors are found, the claim is returned (返戻, henrei) with an error code. The provider must fix the cause and resubmit.
Source: Monthly Cycle Guide
Step 7: Payment (Miyagi schedule)
On the Miyagi kokuho-ren schedule:
  • 15th of M+2: kokuho-ren bills the insurer (municipality)
  • 25th of M+2: insurer pays the kokuho-ren
  • End of M+2: kokuho-ren pays the provider
A visit on April 1 is paid at the end of June. This ~2-month lag affects provider cash flow.
Source: Monthly Cycle Guide
