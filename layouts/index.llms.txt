# {{ site.Title }}

> {{ site.Params.description }}. Published by {{ site.Params.author }}, a Thai care-software company studying Japan's long-term care insurance (介護保険, kaigo hoken) and elderly long-term care in Thailand. Every factual claim is footnoted to a source we opened. Opinions are labelled "Tako-San's take" or "Tako-San's view".

**How this site is organised.** Split by country — Japan and Thailand. For each country: a Start Here series, posts, guides, competitor profiles, and infographics. Shared across both countries: the glossary and the about page.
